## 2. Protocol and Entity Relationship

The Axiom Protocol itself is a set of smart contracts deployed on Arbitrum One (Chain ID 42161). The protocol contracts are not themselves a legal entity. Axiom Nexus LLC provides the following services to the protocol:

- **Identity and compliance operations:** Reviewing KYC submissions, issuing and revoking ERC-3643 claims via the Claim Issuer contract
- **Solvency monitoring:** Producing and publishing solvency snapshots via the Solvency Console
- **Treasury stewardship:** Executing treasury transactions through the Governance Safe (3-of-5 multisig)
- **Reserve management:** Monitoring PSM USDC reserves; initiating governance proposals for reserve rebalancing
- **Regulatory posture monitoring:** Tracking compliance posture under applicable stablecoin and securities frameworks
- **Lending Fund administration:** Acting as fund manager for the SEC Reg D 506(c) lending fund

## 3. Regulatory Framework

### 3.1 AXUSD Stablecoin

Unified AXUSD is structured with reference to the GENIUS Act (Securing and Enabling Commerce Using Stablecoins in the Internet to Nurture the Economy — Public Law 119-27). This reference does not constitute a regulatory compliance certification or legal conclusion. The compliance posture of AXUSD under applicable federal stablecoin law is subject to ongoing legal evaluation. External attestation has not been completed and is pending.

### 3.2 Lending Fund

The Axiom Lending Fund raises capital pursuant to SEC Regulation D, Rule 506(c). This exemption permits general solicitation but restricts participation to verified accredited investors. No offering is made through this disclosure document. Any offering is made only through definitive subscription documents and applicable regulatory procedures.

### 3.3 Protocol Products

Other protocol products — including the SEED participation lockup program, the Wealth Practice savings program, and the land acquisition pipeline — are in various stages of development. Deployed contracts should not be interpreted as operative products available for public financial reliance. See `/disclosure` for the current operational status of each product.

## 4. Token Classification Notice

The AXM governance token and AXUSD stablecoin have not been the subject of any formal regulatory ruling, no-action letter, or registration statement from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), or any other federal or state regulator. The protocol operator maintains the view that:

- AXM is a utility and governance token
- AXUSD is a payment stablecoin designed to align with the GENIUS Act framework

These views are not legal conclusions. Regulatory classification may change based on agency guidance, enforcement actions, or legislative developments. Participants should consult independent legal counsel before acquiring, holding, or transferring any Axiom Protocol tokens.
