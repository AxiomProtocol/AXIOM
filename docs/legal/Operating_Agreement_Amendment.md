# AMENDMENT TO OPERATING AGREEMENT

## AXIOM NEXUS LLC
### Series A: AXUSD Fix & Flip Lending Fund

---

## AMENDMENT NO. 1

This Amendment No. 1 (the "Amendment") to the Operating Agreement of Axiom Nexus LLC (the "Company") is made effective as of [DATE], by and among the Company and its Members.

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company was formed as a Mississippi limited liability company pursuant to an Operating Agreement dated [ORIGINAL DATE] (the "Original Agreement");

WHEREAS, the Members desire to amend the Original Agreement to establish a series of Membership Interests designated as "Series A: AXUSD Fix & Flip Lending Fund" (the "Fund");

WHEREAS, the Fund will operate as a real estate lending fund accepting capital contributions in AXUSD stablecoin and making short-term bridge loans to real estate investors;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Original Agreement is hereby amended as follows:

---

## ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS

The following definitions are added to the Operating Agreement:

**1.1 "Accredited Investor"** means an investor who meets the definition of "accredited investor" under Rule 501(a) of Regulation D.

**1.2 "AXUSD"** means the AXUSD stablecoin, a USD-pegged digital asset operating on the Arbitrum One blockchain at contract address 0x73585df5E62a5E85E6dd6b1df3C08E00eee5b89C.

**1.3 "Capital Contribution"** means the amount of AXUSD contributed by a Member to the Fund.

**1.4 "Fund"** means the Series A: AXUSD Fix & Flip Lending Fund established under this Amendment.

**1.5 "Fund Assets"** means all assets held by the Fund, including AXUSD, loans, and receivables.

**1.6 "Manager"** means Axiom Nexus LLC in its capacity as manager of the Fund.

**1.7 "Member"** means any person admitted as a member of the Fund pursuant to a Subscription Agreement.

**1.8 "Net Profits"** means the net income of the Fund after deduction of all expenses, fees, and reserves.

**1.9 "Preferred Return"** means an 8% annual cumulative return on unreturned Capital Contributions.

**1.10 "Unit"** means a Membership Interest in the Fund, with each Unit representing a $1,000 Capital Contribution.

---

## ARTICLE II: FORMATION OF SERIES A FUND

**2.1 Establishment.** The Fund is hereby established as a series of the Company pursuant to Mississippi Code Section 79-29-1201.

**2.2 Separate Series.** The Fund shall constitute a separate series under Mississippi law. The debts, liabilities, and obligations of the Fund shall be enforceable only against the Fund Assets and not against the assets of any other series or the Company generally.

**2.3 Purpose.** The purpose of the Fund is to:
(a) Pool capital from Accredited Investors;
(b) Originate, fund, and service short-term real estate bridge loans;
(c) Generate returns through interest income and origination fees;
(d) Distribute profits to Members according to this Agreement.

**2.4 Name.** The Fund shall operate under the name "Axiom Nexus LLC - Series A: AXUSD Fix & Flip Lending Fund."

---

## ARTICLE III: CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

**3.1 Minimum Contribution.** The minimum Capital Contribution for each Member is $10,000, payable in AXUSD.

**3.2 Capital Accounts.** The Manager shall maintain a capital account for each Member, reflecting:
(a) Capital Contributions;
(b) Allocations of profits and losses;
(c) Distributions;
(d) Adjustments as required.

**3.3 No Interest.** No interest shall accrue on Capital Contributions.

**3.4 No Withdrawal Right.** Members have no right to withdraw Capital Contributions except as provided in Article VI.

**3.5 AXUSD Settlement.** All Capital Contributions and distributions shall be made in AXUSD. Members are responsible for converting USD to AXUSD and vice versa.

---

## ARTICLE IV: ALLOCATION OF PROFITS AND LOSSES

**4.1 Allocation of Profits.** Net Profits of the Fund shall be allocated as follows:

(a) **First**, 100% to Members until each Member has received allocations equal to their Preferred Return (8% annual cumulative);

(b) **Second**, 100% to Members until each Member has received allocations equal to their unreturned Capital Contributions;

(c) **Third**, 80% to Members (pro rata) and 20% to Manager.

**4.2 Allocation of Losses.** Net Losses shall be allocated to Members in proportion to their Capital Contributions, except that no Member shall have a capital account balance below zero.

**4.3 Tax Allocations.** Tax items shall be allocated in a manner consistent with Treasury Regulation Section 1.704-1(b).

---

## ARTICLE V: DISTRIBUTIONS

**5.1 Timing.** The Manager shall make distributions as follows:
(a) **Monthly:** Interest income collected during the preceding month;
(b) **Quarterly:** Principal returns and catch-up distributions;
(c) **Annually:** Any remaining profits after reserves.

**5.2 Order of Distribution.** Distributions shall be made in the same order as profit allocations under Section 4.1.

**5.3 Reserves.** The Manager may retain reasonable reserves for Fund expenses, loan losses, and contingencies.

**5.4 Distribution Method.** All distributions shall be made in AXUSD to the wallet address designated by each Member.

---

## ARTICLE VI: REDEMPTIONS

**6.1 Lock-Up Period.** No redemptions are permitted during the first 12 months following a Member's initial Capital Contribution (the "Lock-Up Period").

**6.2 Redemption Notice.** After the Lock-Up Period, a Member may request redemption by providing 90 days written notice to the Manager.

**6.3 Redemption Price.** Redemptions shall be made at the Member's capital account balance as of the redemption date.

**6.4 Availability.** Redemptions are subject to available liquidity. The Manager may prorate or delay redemptions if insufficient liquidity exists.

**6.5 Early Redemption Penalty.** Redemptions during the Lock-Up Period (if permitted in Manager's discretion) are subject to a 3% penalty.

**6.6 Queue.** Redemption requests shall be processed in the order received.

---

## ARTICLE VII: MANAGEMENT

**7.1 Manager Authority.** The Manager shall have exclusive authority to manage the Fund, including:
(a) Originating, underwriting, and servicing loans;
(b) Hiring service providers;
(c) Making distributions;
(d) Executing documents on behalf of the Fund;
(e) All other actions necessary for Fund operations.

**7.2 Standard of Care.** The Manager shall manage the Fund with the care, skill, and diligence that a prudent person would exercise.

**7.3 Manager Compensation.** The Manager shall receive:
(a) **Management Fee:** 1.5% annual on committed capital, paid monthly;
(b) **Performance Allocation:** 20% of profits above the Preferred Return;
(c) **Origination Fees:** 50% of origination points collected from borrowers.

**7.4 Expense Reimbursement.** The Fund shall reimburse the Manager for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in Fund operations.

**7.5 Exculpation.** The Manager shall not be liable to Members for any act or omission performed in good faith, except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of fiduciary duty.

**7.6 Indemnification.** The Fund shall indemnify the Manager against any claims arising from Manager's good faith actions on behalf of the Fund.

---

## ARTICLE VIII: MEETINGS AND VOTING

**8.1 Meetings.** The Manager may call meetings of Members as needed. Annual meetings shall be held within 120 days of fiscal year-end.

**8.2 Notice.** Members shall receive 10 days notice of any meeting.

**8.3 Voting.** Members shall vote in proportion to their Capital Contributions.

**8.4 Actions Requiring Member Approval.** The following require approval of Members holding a majority of Capital Contributions:
(a) Removal of Manager;
(b) Amendment of this Agreement affecting Member rights;
(c) Dissolution of the Fund;
(d) Merger or consolidation.

---

## ARTICLE IX: TRANSFERS

**9.1 Restrictions.** Membership Interests may not be transferred without prior written consent of the Manager.

**9.2 Conditions.** The Manager may condition consent on:
(a) Transferee being an Accredited Investor;
(b) Compliance with securities laws;
(c) Payment of transfer costs.

**9.3 Death or Incapacity.** Upon death or incapacity, a Member's interest shall pass to their legal representative, subject to the conditions above.

---

## ARTICLE X: REPORTING

**10.1 Financial Statements.** The Manager shall provide Members with quarterly unaudited financial statements within 45 days of quarter-end.

**10.2 Tax Information.** The Manager shall provide Schedule K-1s within 75 days of fiscal year-end (target: March 15).

**10.3 On-Chain Reporting.** Members may access real-time Fund data via blockchain explorer at the Fund's AXUSD wallet address.

**10.4 Annual Report.** The Manager shall provide an annual report including:
(a) Summary of Fund performance;
(b) Loan portfolio composition;
(c) Default and loss experience;
(d) Manager's outlook.

---

## ARTICLE XI: DISSOLUTION

**11.1 Dissolution Events.** The Fund shall dissolve upon:
(a) Manager election with Member approval;
(b) Judicial dissolution;
(c) Any event making it unlawful to continue.

**11.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Manager shall:
(a) Liquidate Fund Assets;
(b) Pay creditors;
(c) Distribute remaining assets to Members per capital accounts.

---

## ARTICLE XII: MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by Mississippi law.

**12.2 Dispute Resolution.** Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Mississippi.

**12.3 Severability.** Invalid provisions shall be severed without affecting remaining provisions.

**12.4 Entire Agreement.** This Amendment, together with the Original Agreement, the PPM, and Subscription Agreements, constitutes the entire agreement.

**12.5 Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts.
