## GENERAL INVESTMENT RISKS

### 1. LOSS OF PRINCIPAL

**You may lose your entire investment.** There is no guarantee of return of capital. Real estate lending involves substantial risk of loss.

### 2. NO GUARANTEE OF RETURNS

The target returns of 10-14% stated in the Memorandum are not guaranteed. Actual returns may be materially lower than projected, including zero or negative returns.

### 3. ILLIQUIDITY

Your investment is illiquid. There is no public market for the Membership Interests. You may not be able to sell or transfer your interest, and redemptions are subject to significant restrictions including a 12-month lock-up period.

---

## DSCR LENDING SPECIFIC RISKS

### 4. RENTAL INCOME DEPENDENCY

DSCR loans are underwritten based on property rental income. Risks include:
- Rental rates may decline due to market conditions
- Properties may experience extended vacancy
- Tenants may fail to pay rent
- Short-term rental regulations may reduce income
- Economic downturns may reduce rental demand

### 5. DSCR RATIO COMPRESSION

The Debt Service Coverage Ratio may decline after loan origination due to:
- Rental income decreases
- Property tax increases
- Insurance premium increases
- HOA fee increases
- Required repairs or maintenance

### 6. LONG-TERM DURATION MISMATCH

The Fund invests in 30-year amortizing loans. This creates:
- Duration mismatch between assets and potential redemption requests
- Exposure to long-term interest rate changes
- Dependence on continued loan performance over decades
- Potential liquidity constraints for redemptions

### 7. BORROWER DEFAULT

Borrowers may fail to make payments or repay loans due to:
- Property cash flow decline
- Personal financial difficulties
- Property damage or destruction
- Economic hardship
- Strategic default (walking away from underwater properties)

### 8. COLLATERAL VALUE DECLINE

The real estate securing loans may decline in value due to:
- Market downturns
- Property damage
- Neighborhood deterioration
- Environmental issues
- Economic conditions
- Remote work trends affecting housing demand

### 9. FORECLOSURE RISK

If a borrower defaults, the Fund may need to foreclose. Foreclosure is:
- Time-consuming (6-24 months depending on state)
- Expensive (legal fees, property preservation costs)
- Uncertain (recovery may be less than loan amount)
- Complicated by tenant rights and eviction moratoriums

## PROPERTY-SPECIFIC RISKS

### 10. SHORT-TERM RENTAL RISK

For properties relying on short-term rental (Airbnb/VRBO) income:
- Platform policies may change
- Local regulations may restrict or ban STRs
- Occupancy rates may decline
- Competition may increase
- Seasonality may affect income

### 11. MARKET RENT ASSUMPTIONS

For vacant properties underwritten on market rent:
- Actual rental rates may be lower than projected
- Time to find tenants may be longer than expected
- Tenant quality may affect payment reliability
- Market conditions may change before stabilization

### 12. PROPERTY CONDITION

Properties may have undisclosed issues:
- Deferred maintenance
- Foundation or structural problems
- Roof or plumbing issues
- Environmental contamination
- Code violations

### 13. GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION

While the Fund targets nationwide diversification, concentration may occur in certain markets, exposing the portfolio to regional economic risks.

## AXUSD STABLECOIN RISKS

### 14. STABLECOIN PEG RISK

AXUSD is designed to maintain a 1:1 peg with USD. However:
- The peg may temporarily deviate
- In extreme conditions, the peg could break
- You may receive more or less USD value than invested

### 15. SMART CONTRACT RISK

AXUSD and the Fund's vault operate on smart contracts that may contain bugs or vulnerabilities:
- Exploits could result in loss of funds
- Contract upgrades could introduce issues
- Blockchain network failures could disrupt operations

### 16. REGULATORY RISK - STABLECOINS

Stablecoin regulations are evolving rapidly:
- Future regulations may restrict or prohibit AXUSD
- Compliance costs may increase
- The Fund may need to change its settlement currency

### 17. BLOCKCHAIN NETWORK RISK

AXUSD and the Fund operate on Arbitrum One, which may experience:
- Network congestion and delays
- Validator failures
- Security incidents
- Dependence on Ethereum mainnet
- Gas fee fluctuations

### 18. CUSTODY RISK

Members are responsible for their own AXUSD custody:
- Lost private keys cannot be recovered
- Wallet security is your responsibility
- The Fund is not responsible for member custody failures
- Phishing and social engineering attacks are common

## MARKET RISKS

### 27. INTEREST RATE RISK

Rising interest rates may:
- Make refinancing difficult for borrowers
- Reduce property values
- Increase default rates
- Affect new loan origination volume

### 28. ECONOMIC DOWNTURN

Economic recession could cause:
- Reduced property demand
- Falling property values
- Higher vacancy rates
- Increased default rates
- Difficulty selling foreclosed properties

### 29. HOUSING MARKET CORRECTION

A housing market correction could:
- Reduce property values below loan balances
- Increase borrower default rates
- Reduce foreclosure recovery amounts
- Affect rental demand and rates

### 30. COMPETITION

Increased competition in private lending may:
- Compress interest rates
- Reduce origination volume
- Lower quality opportunities
- Increase credit risk as underwriting loosens
