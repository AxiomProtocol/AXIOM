# FORM D FILING GUIDE

## AXIOM NEXUS LLC
### Series B: AXUSD DSCR Rental Lending Fund

---

## OVERVIEW

This guide provides instructions for completing and filing Form D with the SEC for the AXUSD DSCR Rental Lending Fund offering under Rule 506(c) of Regulation D.

---

## FILING REQUIREMENTS

### When to File

Form D must be filed electronically with the SEC within **15 calendar days** after the first sale of securities.

### Where to File

File electronically through the SEC's EDGAR system at: https://www.sec.gov/edgar

### Filing Fee

There is **no filing fee** for Form D.

---

## EDGAR ACCESS

### Obtaining EDGAR Access Codes

Before filing, the Company must obtain EDGAR access codes:

1. **CIK (Central Index Key):** Unique identifier for the Company
2. **EDGAR Access Code:** Used to make filings
3. **Password:** For logging into the EDGAR system

To obtain codes, submit:
- Form ID (Application for EDGAR Access)
- Notarized signature page
- Supporting documentation

Processing time: 2-3 business days

## STATE BLUE SKY FILINGS

### Overview

While Rule 506 offerings are exempt from state registration, many states require notice filings and/or fees.

### States Requiring Notice Filing

Most states require a Form D notice filing with a fee. Common requirements:

| State | Fee | Form Required | Deadline |
|-------|-----|---------------|----------|
| California | $300 | Form D + consent | 15 days after first sale |
| Texas | $500 | Form D | 15 days after first sale |
| Florida | $200 | Form D | 15 days after first sale |
| New York | $300 | Form D | 15 days after first sale |
| [Check each state where investors reside] |

### Filing Process

1. Identify all states where investors are located
2. Research each state's specific requirements
3. Prepare required forms and fees
4. File within required timeframes (usually 15-30 days after first sale in that state)

## AMENDMENT REQUIREMENTS

### When to Amend

File an amendment to Form D:

1. **Annual Amendment:** If offering continues beyond one year, file annual amendment
2. **Material Changes:** Within 15 days of material changes to information
3. **Closing Amendment:** Upon completion of the offering

### Types of Amendments

| Type | When Filed |
|------|------------|
| Amendment | Material changes |
| Annual Update | Each year offering continues |
| Closing | Within 30 days of final sale |

## PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE

### Failure to File

Failure to file Form D may result in:

1. **Loss of Exemption:** Potential loss of Rule 506 exemption for future offerings
2. **State Enforcement:** State securities regulators may take action
3. **Investor Rights:** Investors may have rescission rights
4. **SEC Examination:** Increased scrutiny in future offerings

### Best Practice

File Form D promptly, even if you believe filing may be late. Late filing is generally better than no filing.
