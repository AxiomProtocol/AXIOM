## SECTION 3: THIRD-PARTY VERIFICATION LETTER TEMPLATE

If using third-party verification, the letter must:

1. Be from a registered broker-dealer, SEC-registered investment adviser, licensed attorney, or certified public accountant
2. State that the professional has taken reasonable steps to verify accredited status
3. Be dated within the prior three months
4. Be signed by the professional
