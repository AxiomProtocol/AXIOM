## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### The Opportunity

Axiom Nexus LLC ("Company" or "Fund") is offering Membership Interests to accredited investors to participate in a real estate lending fund focused on short-term bridge loans for residential fix-and-flip projects.

### Investment Highlights

- **Asset Class:** Short-term real estate bridge loans (6-12 months)
- **Target Properties:** Non-owner occupied 1-4 unit residential properties
- **Geographic Focus:** Texas and Florida (initially), expanding to other non-licensing states
- **Settlement Currency:** AXUSD stablecoin (1:1 USD-pegged, blockchain-based)
- **Target Annual Yield:** 10-14% to investors (net of fees)
- **Loan-to-Value:** Maximum 70% of After Repair Value (ARV)
- **Loan Rates to Borrowers:** 12-15% APR + 2-3 points origination

### Why AXUSD Settlement?

All fund operations settle in AXUSD, a compliant USD-pegged stablecoin:
- **Transparency:** All transactions recorded on public blockchain
- **Speed:** Same-day settlement vs. 3-5 day bank transfers
- **Programmable:** Smart contracts automate yield distribution
- **Auditable:** Real-time verification of fund reserves
- **Self-Custody Option:** Investors can hold AXUSD in personal wallets

### Fund Structure

```
Axiom Nexus LLC (Mississippi)
├── Manager: Axiom Nexus LLC (General Partner functions)
├── Members: Accredited Investors (Limited Partner functions)
├── Custody: Smart Contract Vault (on-chain, auditable)
└── Settlement: AXUSD Stablecoin (Arbitrum One blockchain)
```

## 3. BUSINESS STRATEGY & INVESTMENT THESIS

### Market Opportunity

The U.S. fix-and-flip market represents a $2+ trillion opportunity:
- 350,000+ homes flipped annually
- Average profit per flip: $65,000-$80,000
- Traditional banks avoid this market (too short-term, too small)
- Private lending fills the gap at premium rates

### Lending Criteria

The Fund will make loans meeting the following criteria:

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Property Type** | Non-owner occupied 1-4 unit residential |
| **Loan Purpose** | Purchase and/or renovation (business purpose only) |
| **Loan-to-ARV** | Maximum 70% of After Repair Value |
| **Loan-to-Cost** | Maximum 85% of total project cost |
| **Loan Term** | 6-12 months |
| **Interest Rate** | 12-15% annual |
| **Origination Fee** | 2-3 points |
| **Borrower Requirements** | Experience, credit check, property documentation |

### Underwriting Process

1. **Application Review:** Borrower submits deal package
2. **Property Valuation:** Third-party appraisal or BPO for ARV
3. **Borrower Verification:** Credit, experience, background check
4. **Title & Insurance:** Clear title, hazard insurance required
5. **Loan Committee Approval:** Final sign-off by Manager
6. **Closing & Funding:** AXUSD disbursed to borrower within 7-14 days

### Loan Servicing

- Monthly interest payments collected
- Automatic routing to investor distributions
- Property inspections for renovation progress
- Default management and foreclosure if necessary

### Exit Strategies (Borrower)

1. **Sell the property** after renovation
2. **Refinance** into long-term DSCR loan
3. **Pay off** from other capital sources

## 6. RISK FACTORS

**INVESTMENT IN THE COMPANY INVOLVES A HIGH DEGREE OF RISK. PROSPECTIVE INVESTORS SHOULD CAREFULLY CONSIDER THE FOLLOWING RISK FACTORS BEFORE MAKING AN INVESTMENT DECISION.**

### Real Estate Risks

1. **Property Value Decline:** Real estate values may decrease, affecting collateral value and recovery in default.

2. **Borrower Default:** Borrowers may fail to complete renovations, make payments, or repay loans.

3. **Foreclosure Delays:** Foreclosure processes can be lengthy and costly, delaying recovery.

4. **Environmental Hazards:** Properties may have undiscovered environmental issues affecting value.

5. **Market Conditions:** Economic downturns, interest rate changes, or local market conditions may affect property values and borrower ability to sell/refinance.

### Lending Risks

6. **Concentration Risk:** The Fund may have significant exposure to individual borrowers, properties, or geographic areas.

7. **Subordination:** Some loans may be subordinate to senior liens, reducing recovery in default.

8. **Fraud:** Borrowers or third parties may provide fraudulent information or documentation.

9. **Interest Rate Risk:** Rising rates may make refinancing difficult for borrowers.

### Operational Risks

10. **Limited Operating History:** The Fund is newly formed with limited operating history.

11. **Reliance on Manager:** The Fund depends heavily on the Manager's expertise and availability.

12. **Conflicts of Interest:** The Manager may have conflicts between its interests and those of investors.

13. **Technology Risk:** Smart contract bugs, blockchain network issues, or cybersecurity breaches may affect operations.

### AXUSD Stablecoin Risks

14. **Peg Stability:** AXUSD may temporarily deviate from its $1.00 peg.

15. **Regulatory Risk:** Future regulations may affect AXUSD's legality or operations.

16. **Smart Contract Risk:** Bugs in AXUSD smart contracts could result in loss of funds.

17. **Blockchain Network Risk:** Arbitrum One network congestion or failures could delay transactions.

18. **Custody Risk:** Self-custody requires proper security practices by investors.

### Regulatory Risks

19. **Securities Laws:** This offering relies on exemptions that may be challenged.

20. **Lending Regulations:** Changes in state or federal lending laws may affect operations.

21. **Tax Treatment:** Tax laws may change, affecting investor returns.

### Liquidity Risks

22. **No Public Market:** There is no public market for the Membership Interests.

23. **Transfer Restrictions:** Securities cannot be freely transferred without Manager consent.

24. **Redemption Limitations:** Redemptions are subject to available liquidity and Manager discretion.
