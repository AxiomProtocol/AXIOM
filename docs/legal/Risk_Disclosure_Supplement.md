## GENERAL INVESTMENT RISKS

### 1. LOSS OF PRINCIPAL

**You may lose your entire investment.** There is no guarantee of return of capital. Real estate lending involves substantial risk of loss.

### 2. NO GUARANTEE OF RETURNS

The target returns stated in the Memorandum are not guaranteed. Actual returns may be materially lower than projected, including zero or negative returns.

### 3. ILLIQUIDITY

Your investment is illiquid. There is no public market for the Membership Interests. You may not be able to sell or transfer your interest, and redemptions are subject to significant restrictions.

---

## REAL ESTATE LENDING RISKS

### 4. BORROWER DEFAULT

Borrowers may fail to make payments or repay loans. Default rates on fix-and-flip loans can exceed 5-10% in adverse market conditions.

### 5. COLLATERAL VALUE DECLINE

The real estate securing loans may decline in value due to:
- Market downturns
- Property damage
- Neighborhood deterioration
- Environmental issues
- Economic conditions

### 6. FORECLOSURE RISK

If a borrower defaults, the Fund may need to foreclose. Foreclosure is:
- Time-consuming (6-24 months depending on state)
- Expensive (legal fees, property costs)
- Uncertain (recovery may be less than loan amount)

### 7. RENOVATION RISK

Fix-and-flip projects may encounter:
- Cost overruns
- Construction delays
- Contractor failures
- Permit issues
- Unexpected repairs

### 8. GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION

The Fund initially focuses on Texas and Florida, creating geographic concentration risk. Regional economic downturns could disproportionately affect the Fund.

## AXUSD STABLECOIN RISKS

### 9. STABLECOIN PEG RISK

AXUSD is designed to maintain a 1:1 peg with USD. However:
- The peg may temporarily deviate
- In extreme conditions, the peg could break
- You may receive more or less USD value than invested

### 10. SMART CONTRACT RISK

AXUSD operates on smart contracts that may contain bugs or vulnerabilities:
- Exploits could result in loss of funds
- Contract upgrades could introduce issues
- Blockchain network failures could disrupt operations

### 11. REGULATORY RISK - STABLECOINS

Stablecoin regulations are evolving rapidly:
- Future regulations may restrict or prohibit AXUSD
- Compliance costs may increase
- The Fund may need to change its settlement currency

### 12. BLOCKCHAIN NETWORK RISK

AXUSD operates on Arbitrum One, which may experience:
- Network congestion and delays
- Validator failures
- Security incidents
- Dependence on Ethereum mainnet

### 13. CUSTODY RISK

Members are responsible for their own AXUSD custody:
- Lost private keys cannot be recovered
- Wallet security is your responsibility
- The Fund is not responsible for member custody failures
