# SEC FORM D FILING GUIDE

## AXIOM NEXUS LLC
### Rule 506(c) Offering

---

## OVERVIEW

Form D is a brief notice filed with the SEC after you make your first sale of securities under Regulation D. It is NOT an application for approval - it's simply a notice that you have sold (or will sell) securities under the Regulation D exemption.

---

## FILING REQUIREMENTS

### When to File

**File within 15 calendar days after the first sale of securities.**

A "sale" includes:
- Accepting a signed subscription agreement
- Receiving investor funds
- Issuing membership interests

### Where to File

**SEC EDGAR System:** https://www.sec.gov/edgar

### Filing Fee

**$0 - Form D filing is FREE**

### Step 4: State Notice Filings (Blue Sky)

**Mississippi Requirements:**

Mississippi requires notice filing within 15 days of first sale to a Mississippi resident.

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Filing Method | Secretary of State |
| Fee | $300 |
| Form | Form D copy + cover letter |
| Deadline | 15 days after first sale |

**Other States:**

If you accept investors from other states, you must file notice in those states too. Common fees:
- Texas: $0 (no filing required)
- Florida: $0 (no filing required)
- California: $25-300+
- New York: $1,200+

## FORM D FIELD-BY-FIELD GUIDE

### Basic Information

**Item 1: Issuer's Identity**
- Full legal name: Axiom Nexus LLC
- Previous names (if any): None

**Item 2: Principal Place of Business**
- Street address (no P.O. Box)
- City, State, ZIP

**Item 3: Related Persons**
- List all executive officers, directors, and 10%+ beneficial owners
- Include: Name, Address, Relationship to Issuer

**Item 4: Industry Group**
- Select: Real Estate

**Item 5: Issuer Size**
- Select appropriate revenue range
- For new company: $0-$1 million

**Item 6: Federal Exemption(s) Claimed**
- Check: Rule 506(c)
- Do NOT check 506(b) - this is a 506(c) offering

**Item 7: Type of Filing**
- New Notice (first filing)
- Or Amendment (if updating prior filing)

**Item 8: Duration of Offering**
- Select: Longer than one year (if offering continues)

**Item 9: Type(s) of Securities Offered**
- Check: Pooled Investment Fund Interests
- Check: Other (specify: LLC Membership Interests)

**Item 10: Business Combination Transaction**
- Select: No

**Item 11: Minimum Investment**
- Enter: $10,000

**Item 12: Sales Compensation**
- If no broker: Leave blank or enter $0
- If using broker: List recipient name, states, and amounts

**Item 13: Offering and Sales Amounts**
- Total Offering Amount: $5,000,000
- Total Amount Sold: $[Actual amount at filing]
- Total Remaining: $[Difference]
- Check if indefinite: Only if no maximum

**Item 14: Investors**
- Number of Accredited Investors: [Count]
- Number of Non-Accredited Investors: 0 (must be 0 for 506(c))

**Item 15: Sales Commissions & Finders' Fees**
- Enter $0 if none
- List details if applicable

**Item 16: Use of Proceeds**
- Brief description: "Real estate bridge lending to finance purchase and renovation of residential investment properties"

## AMENDMENTS

File an amendment to Form D:
- **Annually** if offering continues beyond 1 year
- When there are **material changes** to information
- When offering is **completed/terminated**

---

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Late Filing:** File within 15 days of first sale
2. **Wrong Exemption:** Make sure to select 506(c), not 506(b)
3. **Missing Related Persons:** Include all officers and 10%+ owners
4. **Non-Accredited Investors:** 506(c) must show 0 non-accredited
5. **State Filings:** Remember to file in investor states too
