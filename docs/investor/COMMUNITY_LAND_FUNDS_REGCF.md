## Executive Overview

### What is Community Land Funds?

Community Land Funds is a first-of-its-kind collective land ownership program that allows everyday Americans—regardless of income or net worth—to invest in strategic land parcels alongside their community. Unlike traditional real estate investing that requires significant capital, Community Land Funds enables participation starting at just **$100 per month**.

### Why This Matters

For generations, land ownership has been a cornerstone of wealth building in America. However, the barriers to entry—large down payments, complex transactions, and institutional gatekeeping—have kept many families locked out of this opportunity.

Community Land Funds changes this by:

- **Lowering the barrier to entry** from tens of thousands of dollars to $100/month
- **Providing SEC-compliant access** to land investment for non-accredited investors
- **Tokenizing ownership** on blockchain for transparency and liquidity
- **Enabling collective governance** over land use and development decisions

### Key Value Proposition

| Traditional Land Investment | Community Land Funds |
|-----------------------------|---------------------|
| $50,000+ minimum | $100/month |
| Accredited investors only | Open to all Americans |
| Paper deeds, opaque ownership | Blockchain-verified ownership tokens |
| Individual decision-making | Community governance |
| Complex legal processes | Simple digital investment |

## SEC Regulation CF Compliance

### What is Regulation CF?

Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF) is an SEC framework that allows companies to raise capital from everyday Americans—not just wealthy "accredited investors." This democratizes investing while maintaining important investor protections.

### Reg CF Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Maximum Raise | $5,000,000 per 12-month period |
| Investor Eligibility | All Americans (with limits) |
| Platform Requirement | Must use SEC-registered platform |
| Disclosure Requirements | Full financial and business disclosures |
| Investment Limits | Based on income and net worth |

### Your Investment Limits

SEC Regulation CF limits how much you can invest based on your financial situation:

**If your annual income OR net worth is LESS than $124,000:**
- You may invest the greater of:
  - $2,500, OR
  - 5% of the lesser of your annual income or net worth

**If BOTH your annual income AND net worth are $124,000 or MORE:**
- You may invest up to 10% of the lesser of your annual income or net worth
- Maximum: $124,000 per 12-month period

### At $100/Month ($1,200/Year)

Most investors will be well within their limits:

| Income Level | Annual Limit | $100/month fits? |
|--------------|--------------|------------------|
| $30,000/year | $2,500 | Yes (48% of limit) |
| $50,000/year | $2,500 | Yes (48% of limit) |
| $75,000/year | $3,750 | Yes (32% of limit) |
| $100,000/year | $5,000 | Yes (24% of limit) |
| $150,000/year | $15,000 | Yes (8% of limit) |

### Investor Protections

1. **KYC Verification** - Identity verification required before investing
2. **Investment Limit Enforcement** - Smart contracts enforce SEC limits
3. **Full Disclosure** - Complete offering documents available
4. **Cancellation Rights** - Cancel investment within specified period
5. **Escrow Protection** - Funds held in escrow until funding target met

## ERC-1155 Token Ownership

### What is ERC-1155?

ERC-1155 is a blockchain token standard that allows multiple types of assets to be managed in a single smart contract. For Community Land Funds, each land parcel has its own token ID, and your ownership is represented by how many tokens of that ID you hold.

### Why ERC-1155?

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Multi-Parcel Support** | Own shares in multiple land parcels with one wallet |
| **Gas Efficiency** | Lower transaction costs than older token standards |
| **Batch Transfers** | Move multiple token types in one transaction |
| **Metadata Support** | Rich property information stored on-chain |
| **Interoperability** | Compatible with wallets, marketplaces, DeFi |

### Your Ownership Tokens

When you invest, you receive tokens that represent:

```
Token Structure:
├─ Token ID: [Unique per land parcel]
├─ Balance: [Number of shares you own]
├─ Total Supply: [Total shares issued for parcel]
├─ Metadata URI: [Link to parcel details on IPFS]
└─ Smart Contract: LandOptionRegistry (verified on Arbitrum)
```

### Token Rights

Each token you hold grants you:

1. **Proportional Ownership** - Your share of the land asset
2. **Governance Voting** - One token = one vote on land decisions
3. **Income Distribution** - Proportional share of any parcel income
4. **Exit Rights** - Ability to sell tokens (subject to liquidity)

### Viewing Your Tokens

Your ownership tokens are visible in:
- MetaMask wallet (add custom token)
- Axiom Protocol investor dashboard
- Arbiscan block explorer
- Any ERC-1155 compatible wallet

## Governance & Stewardship

### Community Governance Model

As a token holder, you have voting rights on key decisions:

| Decision Type | Voting Threshold | Example |
|---------------|-----------------|---------|
| Land Use Plans | Simple majority | Approve farming vs. development |
| Steward Selection | Simple majority | Elect land manager |
| Capital Improvements | 2/3 majority | Build infrastructure |
| Land Sale | 2/3 majority | Sell parcel to third party |

### Steward Corps Program

Land Stewards are community members who take responsibility for managing acquired parcels. They:

1. **Manage day-to-day operations** - Farming, maintenance, security
2. **Execute community decisions** - Implement governance votes
3. **Report to token holders** - Quarterly updates on parcel status
4. **Generate income** - Lease arrangements, agricultural production
5. **Coordinate development** - Oversee improvement projects

### Steward Requirements

- Community member in good standing
- Relevant experience (agriculture, land management, construction)
- Pass background verification
- Complete Steward Corps training program
- Maintain active communication with token holders

### Become a Steward

If you're interested in managing land (not just investing), the Steward Corps program provides training and a path to land stewardship.

## Risk Factors

### Investment Risks

**This is a speculative investment. You could lose your entire investment.**

1. **Market Risk**
   - Land values may decline due to economic conditions
   - Rural land markets can be illiquid
   - Regional factors may negatively impact specific parcels

2. **Liquidity Risk**
   - ERC-1155 tokens may have limited secondary markets
   - You may not be able to sell tokens at your desired price
   - Lock-up periods may apply in certain circumstances

3. **Regulatory Risk**
   - SEC regulations may change
   - State land laws may impose restrictions
   - Cryptocurrency regulations are evolving

4. **Operational Risk**
   - Land management may not meet expectations
   - Stewards may underperform
   - Natural disasters could damage property

5. **Smart Contract Risk**
   - Blockchain vulnerabilities could affect tokens
   - Smart contract bugs are possible despite audits
   - Network congestion may affect transactions

6. **Stablecoin Risk**
   - AXUSD may experience temporary de-pegging
   - Stablecoin regulations are evolving

### Risk Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Market Risk | Conservative land pricing, diverse parcel selection |
| Liquidity Risk | Building secondary market infrastructure |
| Regulatory Risk | SEC Reg CF compliance, legal counsel |
| Operational Risk | Steward training, community oversight |
| Smart Contract Risk | OpenZeppelin standards, code verification |

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Who can invest in Community Land Funds?**
A: Any US resident age 18+ who completes KYC verification. Unlike most real estate investments, you do NOT need to be an accredited investor.

**Q: What is the minimum investment?**
A: $100 per transaction (equivalent to 1 share on most parcels).

**Q: Is there a maximum investment?**
A: Yes, SEC Reg CF limits apply based on your income and net worth. Most investors can invest at least $2,500/year.

**Q: How do I prove I own land?**
A: Your ownership is recorded on the Arbitrum blockchain as ERC-1155 tokens. You can view your tokens in any compatible wallet.

**Q: Can I sell my tokens?**
A: Yes, ERC-1155 tokens are transferable. Secondary market infrastructure is being developed.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What is AXUSD?**
A: AXUSD is Axiom Protocol's stablecoin, pegged 1:1 to the US dollar. All investments and returns are denominated in AXUSD.

**Q: What is Arbitrum One?**
A: Arbitrum is an Ethereum Layer 2 network that provides faster, cheaper transactions while maintaining Ethereum security.

**Q: What if I lose access to my wallet?**
A: Standard Web3 wallet recovery applies. Keep your seed phrase secure. The protocol cannot recover lost wallets.

### Financial Questions

**Q: When do I receive returns?**
A: Returns come from (1) land appreciation when parcels are sold, and (2) income from land use (farming, leasing, timber). Distribution timing varies by parcel.

**Q: Are there any fees?**
A: A 2.5% platform fee is deducted at investment. Gas fees (~$0.10-0.50) apply to blockchain transactions.

**Q: How are taxes handled?**
A: You are responsible for your own tax obligations. Consult a tax professional. Token ownership may create taxable events.

### Safety Questions

**Q: Is my investment insured?**
A: No. Unlike bank deposits, investments in land shares are not FDIC insured and carry risk of loss.

**Q: What happens if Axiom Protocol fails?**
A: Your ownership tokens exist on the blockchain independently. However, land management and governance would need to transition.

**Q: How secure are the smart contracts?**
A: Contracts use OpenZeppelin security standards and are verified on-chain. However, smart contract risk cannot be fully eliminated.
