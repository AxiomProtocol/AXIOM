## Regulatory Framework

### SEC Compliance Structure

| Regulation | Products | Investor Requirements |
|------------|----------|----------------------|
| Reg D 506(c) | Lending Fund, Mortgage Notes, Savings, Rent Streams, Phase 3 Products | Accredited Investors Only |
| Reg CF | Community Land Funds | Open to all investors (with limits) |

### Accredited Investor Qualifications
Per SEC Rule 501(a):
- **Income Test:** $200K+ individual or $300K+ joint income for past 2 years
- **Net Worth Test:** $1M+ net worth (excluding primary residence)
- **Professional:** Series 7, 65, or 82 license holder
- **Entity:** $5M+ in assets, or all owners are accredited

### Compliance Features
- KYC/AML verification on all products
- Investment limit enforcement for Reg CF
- On-chain whitelist for smart contract access
- Immutable audit trail
- Risk disclosures and acknowledgments
- Private Placement Memorandum (PPM) for each offering

## Risk Factors

### Investment Risks

1. **Market Risk:** Real estate values may decline, affecting collateral values
2. **Credit Risk:** Borrowers may default on loans, impacting fund returns
3. **Liquidity Risk:** Investments may have lock-up periods limiting liquidity
4. **Smart Contract Risk:** Potential vulnerabilities in on-chain code
5. **Regulatory Risk:** Changes in securities laws may affect offerings
6. **Stablecoin Risk:** AXUSD may experience temporary de-pegging events

### Mitigating Factors
- Conservative LTV ratios (65-75%)
- Diversified loan portfolios
- Insurance pool coverage for protocol risks
- Third-party audits of smart contracts
- Proactive regulatory compliance approach
