## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. The Myth of the Self-Made Millionaire (2 min)
- "Self-made" is a myth
- Every wealthy person had help
- Mentors, partners, employees, community
- Individualism is an illusion
- True wealth is COLLECTIVE
- Ubuntu: "I am because WE are"

### 2. The History of SUSU (3 min)
- Ancient practice from West Africa
- Rotating savings circle - everyone contributes weekly
- Each week, one person receives the full pot
- No bank needed, no interest, just TRUST
- Brought to the Caribbean, South America, worldwide
- It's how communities have built wealth for centuries

### 3. Why Community Wealth Works (2 min)
- ACCOUNTABILITY: You show up for others, not just yourself
- DISCIPLINE: Group pressure keeps you consistent
- ACCESS: Larger sums than you could save alone
- TRUST: Builds reputation and relationships
- SUPPORT: Celebration and encouragement
- SYNERGY: 1+1 = 3 when we work together

### 4. Modern SUSU with Axiom (2 min)
- Same ancient wisdom, blockchain technology
- Smart contracts enforce the rules
- Transparent - everyone sees everything
- Insurance fund protects against defaults
- Build your AxiomScore - on-chain reputation
- Wealth building for the digital age

### 5. Finding Your Wealth Community (2 min)
- Surround yourself with people who are GROWING
- Find mentors who've achieved what you want
- Join groups focused on building, not complaining
- Your network = your net worth
- SUSU circles are ready-made wealth communities
- You don't have to do this alone
