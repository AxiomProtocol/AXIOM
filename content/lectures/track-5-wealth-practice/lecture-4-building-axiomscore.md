# Lecture 5.4: Building Your AxiomScore

## Duration: 8-12 minutes

---

## HOOK (30 seconds)
"What if you could build a credit score that was based on what you actually DID - not just what you borrowed? Today I'm introducing AxiomScore - your on-chain reputation that proves to the world you're someone who keeps their word."

---

## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. Traditional Credit is Broken (2 min)
- Requires debt to build - backwards!
- Errors are common, hard to fix
- Doesn't reflect your true trustworthiness
- Miss one payment, punished for years
- Controlled by corporations, not you
- There has to be a better way

### 2. What is AxiomScore? (3 min)
- On-chain credit score: 300-850, like traditional credit
- But built on your ACTIONS, not your debt
- SUSU participation: Showing up builds your score
- Payment consistency: On-time every time
- Community engagement: Helping others counts
- Stored on blockchain - transparent, verifiable, YOURS

### 3. How to Build Your Score (2 min)
- Join a SUSU circle and COMPLETE it
- Make every weekly contribution on time
- Never miss a payment
- Help new members get started
- Graduate to larger circles
- Your score grows with your track record

### 4. What Your Score Unlocks (2 min)
- Access to larger SUSU circles
- Lower collateral requirements for DeFi
- Reputation in the Axiom community
- Potential future: Loans, mortgages, opportunities
- Employers, landlords could verify your reliability
- A portable, provable reputation

### 5. Your Score is Your Story (2 min)
- Every on-time payment is a chapter
- Every completed circle is a milestone
- You're writing your financial autobiography
- On-chain, permanent, verifiable
- This is reputation in the digital age
- Build it intentionally

---

## PRACTICAL STEPS
"Here's how to start building your AxiomScore:

1. Join a SUSU circle (start small - $25/week is fine)
2. Set calendar reminders for every payment
3. Automate if possible - never miss
4. Complete the full cycle - don't drop out
5. Graduate to a larger circle
6. Repeat - each cycle builds your score

In 12 months, you'll have a proven track record that speaks for itself."
