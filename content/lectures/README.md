# Axiom Academy Video Lecture Series

## Overview
30 video lectures across 5 tracks for social media and in-person teaching.
Each lecture is designed for 5-15 minute video format.

## Tracks

### Track 1: Financial Foundations (6 lectures)
Transform your relationship with money through practical skills.

### Track 2: Crypto & Blockchain Basics (6 lectures)  
Demystify digital money and blockchain technology.

### Track 3: Prosperity Consciousness (6 lectures)
Rev Ike-inspired teachings on abundance and wealth mindset.

### Track 4: Manifestation & Wealth Mindset (6 lectures)
Spiritual principles of attracting and building wealth.

### Track 5: The Wealth Practice Integration (6 lectures)
Connect mindset to action through SUSU and community.
