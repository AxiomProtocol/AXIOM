## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. What is a Credit Score? (2 min)

- A number between 300-850
- Measures how trustworthy you are with borrowed money
- Lenders, landlords, employers check it
- Higher score = better rates, more opportunities
- Lower score = higher costs, fewer options

### 2. The 5 Factors (3 min)

- Payment History (35%): Pay on time, every time
- Credit Utilization (30%): Use less than 30% of your limit
- Length of History (15%): Keep old accounts open
- Credit Mix (10%): Different types of credit
- New Credit (10%): Don't apply for too much at once
- Focus on the top two - they're 65% of your score

### 3. Credit Utilization Secret (2 min)

- If you have a $1,000 limit, keep balance under $300
- Better: Under $100 (10% utilization)
- Pay BEFORE the statement closes for best results
- High balances hurt even if you pay in full monthly
- Request credit limit increases (don't use them!)

### 4. Building Credit from Scratch (2 min)

- Secured credit card: You deposit $200-500
- Become an authorized user on someone else's card
- Credit builder loans (like what SUSU can help with)
- Pay small recurring bill on the card, pay it off monthly
- 6 months of on-time payments starts building history

### 5. Credit Repair Basics (2 min)

- Get your free report: AnnualCreditReport.com
- Dispute errors in writing
- Negotiate "pay for delete" on collections
- Don't close old cards - it hurts your average age
- Time heals - negative items fall off after 7 years
