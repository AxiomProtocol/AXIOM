# Lecture 1.1: Your Relationship with Money

## Duration: 8-12 minutes

---

## HOOK (30 seconds)
"Before you can build wealth, you have to heal your relationship with money. Today I'm going to help you uncover the money story you've been telling yourself your whole life - and show you how to rewrite it."

---

## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. Money Has a Story (2 min)
- Everyone has a "money story" - beliefs about wealth passed down from family
- Common inherited beliefs:
  - "Money doesn't grow on trees"
  - "Rich people are greedy"
  - "We can't afford that"
  - "Money is the root of all evil"
- These stories become our financial thermostat

### 2. Identify Your Money Story (3 min)
- Ask yourself:
  - What did your parents say about money?
  - What did you learn about wealthy people growing up?
  - What's your first memory involving money?
  - What emotions come up when you think about your bank account?
- Your answers reveal your programming

### 3. The Thermostat Effect (2 min)
- We all have a "financial set point"
- When we earn more, we often unconsciously sabotage to return to our set point
- Examples: unexpected expenses, impulse spending, "treating yourself"
- This isn't lack of discipline - it's programming

### 4. Rewriting Your Story (3 min)
- Step 1: Acknowledge the old story without judgment
- Step 2: Thank it for trying to protect you
- Step 3: Write a NEW money story
- Example: "Money flows to me easily. I am worthy of wealth. I use money to bless my family and community."

---

## STORY TO SHARE
Share a personal example of a limiting money belief you discovered and overcame, or a story of someone who transformed their relationship with money.

---

## CALL TO ACTION
"This week, I want you to write down your money story. Every belief about money you can remember hearing or feeling. Then write a NEW story - the one you're choosing to believe from now on. Drop a comment and tell me one belief you're releasing today."

---

## CLOSING (30 seconds)
"Remember, you're not broken. You were just programmed. And anything that was programmed can be reprogrammed. I'll see you in the next lesson where we talk about the most powerful wealth-building habit: paying yourself first."
