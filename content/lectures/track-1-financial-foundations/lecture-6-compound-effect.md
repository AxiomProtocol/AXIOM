## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. Simple vs Compound Interest (2 min)
- Simple interest: You earn on the original amount only
- Compound interest: You earn on your earnings
- Your money makes money, then THAT money makes money
- It's exponential, not linear
- Time is the magic ingredient

### 2. The Math That Changes Everything (3 min)
- $200/month at 7% annual return:
  - After 10 years: $34,000
  - After 20 years: $104,000
  - After 30 years: $244,000
  - After 40 years: $525,000
- You only put in $96,000 over 40 years
- Compound interest added $429,000
- START EARLY

### 3. The Penny Doubled (2 min)
- Would you rather have $1 million today OR a penny doubled daily for 30 days?
- Day 1: $0.01
- Day 10: $5.12
- Day 20: $5,242
- Day 30: $5.3 MILLION
- This is compound growth

### 4. Why Starting NOW Matters (2 min)
- Person A: Saves $200/month from age 25-35, then stops (10 years, $24,000 invested)
- Person B: Saves $200/month from age 35-65 (30 years, $72,000 invested)
- At age 65:
  - Person A: $338,000
  - Person B: $244,000
- Person A invested LESS but has MORE
- Time beats amount

### 5. Apply This to Everything (2 min)
- Compound interest works on habits too
- 1% better every day = 37x better in a year
- Small disciplines repeated = massive results
- SUSU circles: Small consistent contributions compound
- Consistency over intensity
