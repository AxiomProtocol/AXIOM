# Lecture 2.4: Your First Wallet - Owning Your Keys

## Duration: 8-12 minutes

---

## HOOK (30 seconds)
"Not your keys, not your crypto. You've probably heard that phrase. Today I'm showing you how to set up your first crypto wallet - and why owning your own keys is the difference between financial freedom and just another bank account."

---

## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. What is a Crypto Wallet? (2 min)
- Doesn't actually "hold" crypto
- Holds your KEYS to access crypto on blockchain
- Like a keychain, not a vault
- Public key: Your address (like email address)
- Private key: Your password (NEVER share)

### 2. Types of Wallets (2 min)
- Custodial (Exchange wallets)
  - Coinbase, Binance hold your keys
  - Convenient but you don't truly own it
  - "Not your keys, not your crypto"
- Non-Custodial (Self-custody)
  - YOU hold the keys
  - MetaMask, Trust Wallet (software)
  - Ledger, Trezor (hardware)

### 3. Setting Up MetaMask (3 min)
- Download from official site (metamask.io)
- Create new wallet
- WRITE DOWN your seed phrase (12 words)
- This is your master key - never digital, never shared
- Store it like cash - fire safe, multiple copies
- Verify by writing it again

### 4. Seed Phrase Security (2 min)
- These 12 words = access to all your money
- NEVER:
  - Screenshot them
  - Store in notes app
  - Email them to yourself
  - Share with "support"
- If anyone asks for your seed phrase = SCAM
- Lose the phrase = lose your crypto forever

### 5. Your First Transaction (2 min)
- Copy your public address
- Send a small test amount first
- Wait for confirmation
- Check block explorer to see it
- Congratulations - you're self-sovereign
