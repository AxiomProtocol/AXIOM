# Lecture 3.3: Money Loves Me - Prosperity Affirmations

## Duration: 8-12 minutes

---

## HOOK (30 seconds)
"Money is like a person - it goes where it's appreciated and welcomed. If you keep saying 'I'm broke,' money hears you and stays away. Today I'm teaching you the affirmations that make money LOVE you and want to be with you."

---

## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. What Are Affirmations? (2 min)
- Positive statements spoken as if already true
- You're already affirming all day - mostly negatively
- "I'm broke." "I can't afford that." "Money is tight."
- These are NEGATIVE affirmations
- Time to replace them with WEALTH affirmations

### 2. Why Affirmations Work (2 min)
- Words shape thoughts, thoughts shape reality
- Repetition rewires neural pathways
- Speaking activates more brain regions than thinking
- Your subconscious doesn't know true from false
- Feed it wealth, it creates wealth conditions
- Rev Ike: "Talk yourself into prosperity"

### 3. The Core Money Affirmations (3 min)
- "Money loves me, and I love money"
- "I am a money magnet"
- "Wealth flows to me easily and abundantly"
- "I always have more than enough"
- "I am worthy of receiving unlimited wealth"
- "I release all resistance to money"
- Say them with FEELING, not just words

### 4. How to Use Them (2 min)
- Morning: First thing, before your feet hit the floor
- Mirror: Look yourself in the eyes while speaking
- Throughout day: Replace negative money talk instantly
- Before bed: Last thoughts before sleep are powerful
- Write them: Reinforce with journaling
- Speak with conviction, not doubt

### 5. The "I AM" Power (2 min)
- "I AM" are the two most powerful words
- Whatever follows "I am" you become
- "I am broke" = broke identity
- "I am wealthy" = wealthy identity
- Your subconscious accepts "I am" statements as commands
- Command yourself into prosperity

---

## AFFIRMATION PRACTICE
Lead the audience through these affirmations, speaking with power:

"Repeat after me:
- I AM wealthy. I AM prosperous. I AM abundant.
- Money loves me, and I love money.
- I am a MAGNET for wealth.
- Everything I touch turns to gold.
- I DESERVE to be rich, and I AM rich.
- Thank you, God, for my abundant prosperity."

---

## CALL TO ACTION
"Pick your three favorite money affirmations. Write them on your mirror, your phone wallpaper, anywhere you'll see them. Say them 10 times each, morning and night, for 30 days. Watch your financial reality shift. Comment below your favorite affirmation."

---

## CLOSING (30 seconds)
"Words are seeds. Plant wealth. Speak prosperity. Talk yourself rich. The money is coming - your job is to believe it's already here. Next lesson: Giving to Get - how generosity opens the floodgates of abundance."
