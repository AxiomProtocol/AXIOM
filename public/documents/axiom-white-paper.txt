I. ABSTRACT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Wealth creation in the modern era runs on infrastructure. Not
ideas. Not intentions. Infrastructure — the rails, instruments,
and institutions through which capital flows, compounds, and
transfers across generations. That infrastructure has been
deliberately and systematically built for a narrow slice of
the world's population.

Axiom Protocol is a blockchain-native infrastructure protocol
that rewrites this dynamic — not through redistribution, but
through access. We are constructing a sovereign digital-physical
economy: a governed, modular, and institutionally-anchored protocol
stack that connects programmable on-chain financial rails to
real-world assets, community wealth mechanics, compliant lending
facilities, and transparent treasury operations.

The protocol is live on Arbitrum One and governed in real time
by AXM token holders. Its core instruments — AXUSD, an ERC-3643
compliant stablecoin, and AXAU, a PAXG-backed programmable gold
reserve — anchor a broader ecosystem that includes a live land
acquisition pipeline, community rotating credit (The Wealth
Practice), a multi-strategy treasury secured by the Sentinel
authorization engine, DePIN physical infrastructure integration,
and a suite of Reg D 506(c) compliant lending and capital products.

This paper describes how these layers work, how they connect
to one another, and why the architecture is designed the way it is.

II. THE PROBLEM: INFRASTRUCTURE THAT WAS NEVER BUILT FOR YOU
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 The Wealth Infrastructure Gap

The tools that generate and compound durable wealth — diversified
real asset portfolios, gold-backed instruments, rotating credit
facilities, institutional lending markets, and governed treasury
management — have never been available to most people. Not because
most people lack the intelligence or discipline to use them. Because
the infrastructure was never built for them.

This is not an accident. Financial infrastructure reflects the
interests of those who build it. Institutions build tools for
institutions. The result is a system where access to wealth
creation compounds in one direction — toward those who already
hold capital — and closes itself off from those who do not.

Blockchain technology promised to change this. In many ways,
it has succeeded in creating new primitives: permissionless
lending, self-custody assets, transparent liquidity pools,
and programmable settlement. But it has largely failed to
connect those primitives to physical reality, build compliance
infrastructure rather than avoid it, or create wealth mechanics
that reflect community rather than just capital.

2.2 The DeFi Gap

Decentralized finance has created real access to financial
primitives. But most DeFi protocols are optimized for those
who already have crypto assets and technical literacy. They are
not designed for the communities that need financial infrastructure
most urgently. They do not connect to real estate, real land, or
real community wealth practices. They treat regulatory compliance
as an enemy rather than a design requirement. And they are governed,
in most cases, by founder teams and early token holders whose
interests may not align with those of the broader community.

2.3 The Real Asset Promise, Mostly Unfulfilled

The "real-world asset tokenization" narrative has been prominent
in blockchain for years. But the actual delivery of accessible,
community-governed, real-asset-backed instruments has lagged
dramatically behind the narrative. Most "tokenized asset" projects
are either institutional-only, theoretical, or structurally similar
to the same closed-access systems they claim to be disrupting.

Axiom Protocol is the answer to this gap — built from the ground up
to be governed, real-asset-backed, compliance-aware, and accessible
to the communities that have historically been shut out.

III. THE VISION: A SOVEREIGN DIGITAL-PHYSICAL ECONOMY

3.1 What "Sovereign" Means Here

Sovereignty is not about nationality or politics. In the context
of Axiom Protocol, sovereignty means three specific things:

  SELF-CUSTODY   Your assets are yours. AXAU lives in your wallet,
                 not our custody account. AXUSD is transferred
                 through your wallet, not through our bank account.
                 Where technical constraints require custody —
                 such as BitGo CaaS for treasury assets — it is
                 institutional-grade, multi-party authorized, and
                 disclosed transparently.

  GOVERNANCE     No hidden admin keys. No founder multisig.
                 Protocol parameters, capital allocation, and
                 upgrade decisions are made by AXM holders
                 through on-chain governance. The protocol
                 serves its participants, not its builders.

  REAL BACKING   The word "backed" is used precisely here.
                 AXAU is backed by PAXG — a token that
                 represents one troy ounce of physical gold.
                 The land pipeline targets real properties
                 and issues on-chain receipts for real
                 acquisitions. This is not synthetic exposure
                 or algorithmic backing.

3.2 Digital-Physical Integration

The "digital-physical" in our name describes a deliberate design
choice: that the protocol must connect the digital economy to the
physical world in ways that are meaningful and real — not just
in marketing.

  Digital:       On-chain settlement, programmable instruments,
                 governance tokens, automated authorization,
                 yield markets, identity compliance.

  Physical:      Real estate properties, troy ounces of gold,
                 DePIN nodes providing actual storage infrastructure,
                 community rotating credit circles with real
                 participants making real contributions.

The integration between these layers — through oracle pricing,
on-chain asset receipts, DePIN node registration, and community
credit scoring — is what distinguishes Axiom Protocol from
both traditional finance and from most DeFi projects.

3.3 The Four Pillars

Everything Axiom Protocol builds rests on four structural pillars:

  PILLAR I — PROGRAMMABLE SETTLEMENT
  AXUSD is the protocol's base settlement layer: a compliant,
  yield-bearing stablecoin that can be used across the full
  protocol stack — lending, savings, treasury operations,
  community credit, and real asset settlement.

  PILLAR II — HARD ASSET RESERVES
  AXAU (gold) and KAG (silver) are programmable reserve
  instruments that hold value outside the digital asset
  ecosystem. They are redeemable for the physical assets
  that back them, not just their dollar equivalent.

  PILLAR III — REAL ASSET ONBOARDING
  The land pipeline and DePIN integration create two pathways
  for physical assets to enter the protocol's on-chain registry:
  real estate through a structured acquisition and issuance
  process, and digital infrastructure through DePIN node
  registration and governance.

  PILLAR IV — COMMUNITY WEALTH MECHANICS
  The Wealth Practice formalizes rotating credit on-chain.
  Community Credit extends credit access based on participation.
  These systems are rooted in community traditions that
  predate institutional finance — not invented by a protocol team.

IV. PROTOCOL ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Design Philosophy

Axiom Protocol is designed as a modular system with a unified
governance layer. Each module can operate independently but is
designed to be composable with the others. The protocol does not
require participants to use every layer — a community user can
engage with the Wealth Practice without holding AXAU, and an
allocator can hold AXAU without participating in the land pipeline.

But the layers become more powerful when combined. Community credit
earned through the Wealth Practice opens access to the lending fund.
AXUSD earned through the savings layer can be deployed into Euler V2
yield markets. AXM governance determines which land assets enter
the registry. Every layer feeds the others.

4.2 The capinfra Backend

The protocol's infrastructure backbone is capinfra — a modular
monolith that manages the asset registry, identity layer, policy
engine, market data ingestion, and audit event recording. Settlement
adapters route transactions through INTERNAL, STELLAR, PLAID, and
EVM settlement paths depending on the nature of each operation.

capinfra is not a public API. It is the internal coordination layer
that ensures every on-chain action is recorded, every identity check
is applied, and every policy parameter is enforced consistently.

4.3 Smart Contract Architecture (Arbitrum One)

The protocol's smart contract layer handles:

  Token contracts:       AXUSD (ERC-20 + ERC-3643), AXM (ERC-20)
  Treasury vault:        Multi-strategy ERC-4626 vault
  StrategyManager:       Routes capital between Aave V3 and Camelot
  Parity Stability Module: Maintains AXUSD peg integrity
  Oracle layer:          ERC-7726 pricing | LandNAVOracle for land
  Identity registry:     ERC-3643 on-chain identity enforcement
  NFT instruments:       AxiomFounderBadge | AxiomParticipation
                         | AxiomLandReceipt
  Governance:            AXM staking, emissions, and voting registry

4.4 Data Architecture

  MongoDB                    Analytics and time-series data

4.5 Instrumentation Layer

On every application cold start, an idempotent instrumentation
process runs. It creates any missing database tables, indexes,
sequences, and schema objects — ensuring that production always
matches the protocol's current schema definition without
requiring destructive migration scripts. This design choice
eliminates an entire category of production deployment risk.

V. AXUSD — THE SETTLEMENT STABLECOIN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 The Design Choice: Compliance First

When most stablecoin teams think about compliance, they think
about legal disclaimers and off-chain processes that can be
switched off if regulators look away. Axiom Protocol took a
fundamentally different approach: encode compliance into the
contract itself.

AXUSD implements ERC-3643 — the T-REX standard for compliant
digital securities and tokens. This means:

  - Identity verification is enforced at the contract level,
    not the application layer. An address that has not completed
    KYC cannot receive AXUSD, full stop.
  - Transfer restrictions are programmable and governed.
    Compliance rules can be updated through governance votes,
    not by a founder with an admin key.
  - Identity expiry is monitored automatically. A daily cron
    process checks for expiring KYC records and flags them
    for renewal before they affect participant access.

This architecture makes AXUSD genuinely compliance-ready for
the regulatory environment that is emerging — not just the
environment that existed when it was designed.

5.2 The Parity Stability Module

The PSM maintains AXUSD's peg through automated control layers
that monitor collateral ratios, mint and burn dynamics, and
reserve backing in real time. The Active Contract Verification
System runs continuous integrity checks on PSM contracts and
triggers alerts on any deviation from defined parameters.

This is not a passive mechanism. The PSM is actively managed
and monitored, with full audit trails of every intervention.

5.3 Euler V2 Yield Integration

AXUSD is integrated with Euler V2 lending markets on Arbitrum One.
Participants who supply AXUSD earn variable yield determined by
utilization rates in the lending pool. This yield is real and
on-chain. It is also variable — utilization rates change with
market conditions, and no yield figure is guaranteed.

5.4 GENIUS Act Alignment

AXUSD is designed to align with the principles of the GENIUS Act
framework for payment stablecoin issuers. The protocol uses the
phrase "designed to align with" deliberately — not "compliant with"
— because legal compliance determinations require jurisdiction-specific
legal analysis that each participant must conduct independently.

5.5 Settlement Across the Protocol

AXUSD is the common settlement currency across the Axiom stack:
lending fund contributions, savings positions, community credit
flows, treasury operations, and real asset settlement all
denominate in AXUSD. This creates a coherent monetary layer
across the entire protocol ecosystem.

VI. AXAU — THE GOLD RESERVE INSTRUMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1 Why Gold

Gold has been a store of value for over five thousand years.
It is not correlated with most financial assets. It is not
controlled by any central bank. It does not require counterparty
trust to maintain its intrinsic properties. And it has historically
served as a hedge against currency debasement and inflation —
two forces that disproportionately affect communities with
the least access to inflation-protected assets.

For all of these reasons, gold belongs in any serious wealth
infrastructure stack. AXAU is how it gets there, on-chain,
in self-custody, accessible through Stripe or a Coinbase
card swipe.

6.2 How AXAU Works

AXAU is backed by PAXG — the Paxos Gold token, where each
unit represents one troy ounce of physical gold stored in
LBMA-accredited vaults. PAXG itself is one of the most
credible physical gold representations in digital markets.
AXAU builds on that foundation, adding:

  - Arbitrum One settlement (faster and cheaper than mainnet)
  - ERC-7726 oracle pricing for accurate on-chain valuation
  - LandNAVOracle integration for protocol-wide asset pricing
  - Stripe and Coinbase Onramp fiat entry rails
  - BitGo CaaS custody for treasury-held reserves

6.3 The Redemption Design

This is a critical design point: AXAU redemption returns PAXG,
not USD. This is intentional. We do not want to add a fiat
conversion step that introduces counterparty risk, banking
dependency, and regulatory friction at the moment a participant
wants to realize the value of their gold holding.

Returning PAXG means your gold stays programmable. You received
a digital representation of physical gold. You can redeem it
for a digital representation of physical gold that you can
then hold, trade, or convert on your own terms.

6.4 Fiat Entry: What Is and Is Not Live

The AXAU launch is a crypto-native, Stripe and Coinbase fiat
entry launch. The following are active:

  ACTIVE:    Stripe (card/payment), Coinbase Onramp (card-to-crypto),
             Arbitrum One settlement, BitGo CaaS custody

  DEFERRED:  ACH transfers, wire transfers, virtual bank accounts,
             direct deposit, rent collection, payroll, fiat
             redemption to bank accounts

This is not a limitation. It is a deliberate sequencing decision.
The crypto-native launch proves the settlement infrastructure
before adding the complexity of traditional banking rails.

6.5 KAG — Silver Reserve

KAG is the silver reserve instrument, positioned as the natural
pair to AXAU in the protocol's hard asset reserve layer. It
follows the same structural architecture with silver as the
physical reference asset, and is listed as a first-class
instrument in the protocol stack.

7.1 What Governance Actually Means Here

Governance is one of the most overused words in blockchain. In
most protocols, "governance" means a token holder can submit
proposals that a core team then chooses whether to implement.
In Axiom Protocol, governance means AXM holders actually control
the protocol — structurally, not just rhetorically.

There are no admin keys. There are no founder override mechanisms.
Protocol parameters, treasury allocation policy, asset onboarding
decisions, bridge allowlists, and upgrade authorizations all
require on-chain AXM governance votes. If the governance community
doesn't approve it, it doesn't happen.

7.2 What AXM Holders Govern

  TREASURY POLICY         Target allocation percentages per bucket,
                          rebalancing triggers, strategy parameters

  ASSET ONBOARDING        Which land properties enter the registry,
                          which DePIN nodes are authorized, which
                          commodities are added to the protocol stack

  PROTOCOL PARAMETERS     Fee structures, yield distribution,
                          PSM parameters, identity requirements

  EMISSIONS SCHEDULES     AXM staking yield rates, lockup periods,
                          distribution to protocol contributors

  BRIDGE ALLOWLIST        Which bridge contracts are authorized
                          for cross-chain asset movement

  UPGRADE AUTHORIZATION   Any structural contract upgrade requires
                          a multi-party governance vote — no single
                          actor can unilaterally upgrade the protocol

7.3 Staking and Participation Lockup

AXM holders can participate in "participation lockup" — the
protocol's term for staking — to earn variable yield from
protocol emissions while signaling long-term alignment.
Lockup parameters are governed by AXM holders themselves.
No fixed rate is promised. Yield is variable and emission-dependent.

7.4 The L3 Migration Path

AXM is currently on Arbitrum One. The planned migration to
Universe Blockchain — an Arbitrum Orbit L3 — will enable
higher governance throughput, lower transaction costs, and
protocol-native cross-chain interoperability. The migration
requires AXM governance authorization and will be executed
with full continuity guarantees for all existing positions.

8.2 Axiom Sentinel

Every capital movement through the Axiom Treasury is gated by
Sentinel — the protocol's capital risk and authorization engine.

The process is deliberately two-step:

  STEP 1   Sentinel evaluates current market conditions, treasury
           health, and allocation parameters. If conditions pass,
           it issues a cryptographically signed, 5-minute
           authorization token. This token is non-transferable
           and time-bound.

  STEP 2   The authorization token is presented to execute the
           on-chain rebalance. No valid token means no execution.
           No exceptions.

Authorization nonces are persisted in the database to prevent
replay attacks across server instances. The system is designed
to fail safely — if Sentinel cannot evaluate conditions, no
capital moves.

8.3 MIRDT Capital Intelligence Terminal

The MIRDT Terminal is the protocol's macro-regime detection and
capital intelligence system. It combines the Adaptive Metrics
Engine (AME) — a real-time allocation signal processor — with
an AI Oracle powered by Google Gemini for narrative-aware market
interpretation. MIRDT outputs inform Sentinel evaluation but
do not override governance-authorized policy parameters.

Think of MIRDT as the protocol's strategic intelligence layer
and Sentinel as its authorization layer. Strategy flows from
intelligence. Authorization enforces strategy. Capital moves
only when both are satisfied.

8.4 BitGo CaaS Custody

Treasury assets are held in BitGo CaaS — institutional-grade
custody with multi-party authorization required for any asset
movement. No single key can unilaterally access treasury funds.
This is the same custody infrastructure used by major institutions
and exchanges. We use it because protocol participants deserve
the same level of custodial protection that institutions receive.

8.5 Axiom Rail

Axiom Rail is a Stellar SEP-compliant payment infrastructure
layer that enables cross-border settlement for protocol
transactions. For participants in jurisdictions where
Arbitrum One settlement creates friction, Axiom Rail provides
an alternative settlement path that maintains full audit trails
and protocol connectivity.

IX. THE LAND PIPELINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

9.1 Land as a Protocol Layer

Real estate is the world's largest asset class. It is also one
of the most powerful mechanisms for intergenerational wealth
transfer. And it is almost entirely inaccessible to communities
without existing capital, credit history, or institutional
relationships.

Axiom Protocol does not promise to solve real estate access through
a single product. It is building the infrastructure layer — the
pipeline, the underwriting tools, the on-chain issuance mechanism,
and the governance framework — through which real property can be
identified, acquired, recorded, and governed on-chain.

9.2 The Six-Stage Pipeline

  STAGE 1 — IDENTIFICATION
  The Distressed Property Feed surfaces acquisition opportunities
  through data-driven analysis of market conditions, property
  distress signals, and strategic alignment with the protocol's
  land acquisition framework.

  STAGE 2 — ANALYSIS
  Every property that passes Stage 1 is analyzed through the
  Property Analysis Tool, which integrates RentCast API for
  rental market comparables and Walk Score API for location
  quality scoring. AI-powered document ingestion (Gemini)
  processes property records, title documents, and inspection
  reports into structured data.

  STAGE 3 — UNDERWRITING
  The IVCEE (Investment Value and Capital Efficiency Engine)
  produces formal underwriting memos for each property.
  The AI Acquisition Memo Builder generates structured investment
  summaries formatted for governance review.

  STAGE 4 — GOVERNANCE AUTHORIZATION
  No capital is committed to a property without an AXM
  governance vote. The governance community reviews the
  IVCEE underwriting memo, the AI acquisition summary, and
  the Property Analysis output before voting. If the vote
  fails, the property does not proceed. Governance is not
  a rubber stamp — it is a structural gate.

  STAGE 5 — FIELD CAPTURE (LAYER 5)
  Upon governance authorization, the Field Capture System
  initiates structured on-site data collection: photography,
  condition assessments, utility records, environmental data,
  and neighborhood context. This data is linked to the
  property's record in the protocol's asset registry.

  STAGE 6 — ON-CHAIN ISSUANCE
  Upon acquisition, an AxiomLandReceipt NFT is issued on
  Arbitrum One. This NFT is the on-chain record of the
  acquired property — linked to the asset registry, priced
  through LandNAVOracle, and permanently associated with
  the acquisition documentation.

9.3 Full Lifecycle Tracking

The protocol tracks every acquired property through its full
lifecycle:

  Acquisition        Date, price, strategy type, condition grade
  Rehabilitation     Scope, cost, timeline, contractor records
  Disposition        Sale price, outcome vs. underwriting model
  Variance Analysis  Actual vs. projected return, deviation analysis
  Lessons Learned    Governance-facing post-mortem for each asset

This creates a permanent, auditable, and improving record of
real asset operations — one that makes every subsequent acquisition
more informed than the last.

X. THE WEALTH PRACTICE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

10.1 An Ancient System With Modern Infrastructure

Across cultures and continents, communities have used rotating
savings and credit associations to pool capital and provide
lump-sum access to funds that no individual member could
accumulate alone. In West Africa, it is called "susu." In the
Caribbean, "partner." In East Asia, "hui." In Latin America,
"tanda." In Ethiopia, "iqub." In India, "chit fund."

These systems share a fundamental architecture: a group of trusted
members contribute a fixed amount on a regular schedule, and each
cycle one member receives the full pool. The rotation continues
until every member has received. The system requires no credit
score, no collateral, no bank account, and no institutional
intermediary. It requires community trust — which many communities
have in abundance.

Axiom Protocol's Wealth Practice is not an attempt to reinvent
this system. It is an attempt to formalize it on infrastructure
that eliminates the limitations of the informal version — without
destroying the community logic that makes it work.

10.2 What the On-Chain Version Enables

When rotating credit operates entirely on trust and handshake
agreements, several limitations emerge:
  - It cannot scale beyond the trust radius of a single community
  - It generates no formal credit history that benefits members
    in other financial contexts
  - Disputes have no formal resolution mechanism
  - Members who move or disconnect from the community lose access

The on-chain version addresses each of these:

  SCALE         Groups can form and operate without geographic
                proximity. Protocol rules enforce the mechanics.

  CREDIT RECORD Participation history is permanently recorded
                on-chain and used to generate Community Credit
                scores that unlock protocol lending access.

  DISPUTE RESOLUTION  Protocol rules are enforced by automated
                      control layers, not by group consensus alone.

  PORTABILITY   Membership and credit history persist on-chain
                and are not lost when members move or change
                their social context.

10.3 Connection to the Protocol Ecosystem

The Wealth Practice is not a standalone product. It is a gateway
into the full Axiom Protocol ecosystem:

  COMMUNITY CREDIT     Participation history generates credit
                       scores that unlock protocol lending access
                       without requiring traditional creditworthiness

  GOVERNANCE ACCESS    Active Wealth Practice participants gain
                       pathways to AXM governance participation

  SAVINGS INTEGRATION  Members can direct Wealth Practice receipts
                       into AXUSD savings positions automatically

  LAND PIPELINE ACCESS Future governance votes may allocate land
                       pipeline capacity to Wealth Practice groups
                       as a community wealth building mechanism

XI. LENDING, YIELD, AND CAPITAL PRODUCTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

11.1 The Lending Fund

The Axiom Lending Fund is a private placement vehicle structured
under SEC Regulation D, Rule 506(c). Under 506(c), general
solicitation is permitted — the protocol can publicly discuss
the fund's existence — but participation is restricted to
verified accredited investors.

The fund deploys capital into Euler V2 AXUSD Lending Markets
on Arbitrum One. Yield is variable and determined by utilization
rates. This is not a promise of any specific return.

Participants must complete identity verification before accessing
the fund. This is not bureaucratic friction — it is the legal
requirement for 506(c) participation, and the protocol's ERC-3643
identity infrastructure makes it significantly less friction than
traditional private placement processes.

11.2 Savings

On-chain savings positions connected to the AXUSD yield
infrastructure. Variable rate. The savings product is designed
for protocol participants who want exposure to AXUSD yield
without the accredited investor requirements of the lending fund.

11.3 Community Credit

Community Credit is a protocol-native credit facility that uses
Wealth Practice participation history as its primary credit signal.
This is a deliberate design choice: we want to extend credit access
based on demonstrated community participation and reliability —
not based on the credit bureau scores that encode historical
financial exclusion.

Participants with strong Wealth Practice participation records
can access Community Credit without traditional collateral
requirements. The credit facility operates within the AXUSD
settlement layer.

11.4 The Syndication Module

The Syndication Module enables SEC Reg D 506(c) compliant capital
raises for real estate and infrastructure investments that connect
to the protocol's land pipeline and asset registry. The Investor
Portal provides verified accredited investors with structured
access to deal documentation, underwriting summaries, and
participation mechanics.

11.5 Earn AXUSD

The Earn AXUSD layer is a bootstrap yield program for early AXUSD
allocators, designed to incentivize initial liquidity in the AXUSD
ecosystem. Yield is variable and governed by the emissions schedule
approved through AXM governance.

All yield products referenced in this section carry variable rates.
No return is guaranteed. Participants should read all applicable
disclosures before participating.

XII. DEPIN INTEGRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

12.1 Physical Infrastructure as a Protocol Asset Class

The "digital-physical" vision of Axiom Protocol extends beyond
real estate. Just as land can be acquired, documented, and governed
on-chain, so can digital physical infrastructure — the storage
nodes, compute resources, and connectivity infrastructure that
underpin the digital economy.

Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (DePIN) represent
the application of blockchain governance to physical infrastructure
ownership. Participants who operate infrastructure nodes earn
protocol rewards for providing real services — not for holding
tokens passively.

12.2 DeNet Integration

Axiom Protocol's first DePIN integration is with DeNet — a
decentralized storage network where participants provide storage
capacity to the network and earn in return. The integration:

  - Allows protocol participants to register DeNet nodes as
    assets in the Axiom Protocol asset registry
  - Connects node performance data to the protocol's
    analytics layer
  - Enables governance votes on DePIN infrastructure policy
  - Creates a pathway for DePIN rewards to flow into
    AXUSD-denominated protocol positions

12.3 Why DePIN Belongs in This Stack

The Wealth Practice gives communities access to credit. The land
pipeline gives communities access to real estate. DePIN gives
communities access to ownership of the infrastructure layer
of the digital economy. These are not separate ideas — they are
the same idea applied to different asset classes.

A community that owns land, holds gold reserves, participates in
governed lending markets, and operates digital infrastructure
nodes is not dependent on any single institution or market.
That is what sovereignty looks like in practice.

13.1 Arbitrum One: Today's Home

All production infrastructure operates on Arbitrum One. The choice
of Arbitrum One reflects a deliberate tradeoff: EVM compatibility
for maximum tooling and integration support, an established DeFi
ecosystem (Aave V3, Camelot, Euler V2), and institutional
familiarity for allocator-facing products.

13.2 Universe Blockchain (L3): Tomorrow's Home

Universe Blockchain is an Arbitrum Orbit L3 planned as the
protocol's long-term home. An L3 built on Arbitrum's stack provides:

  - Protocol-native transaction fee structure
    (fees can be denominated in AXUSD)
  - Higher throughput for governance and settlement operations
  - Custom precompiles for identity and compliance functions
  - Reduced bridging surface area and attack vectors

The migration to Universe Blockchain will be authorized by AXM
governance and executed with full continuity for all participant
positions. No position will be lost or stranded in the migration.

13.3 Sui Blockchain: Community Layer

Axiom Protocol is building a community rewards claim layer on
the Sui blockchain, structurally separate from the core
financial infrastructure. This layer issues AXIOM_MAINNET_CLAIM
tokens — community coordination instruments with no monetary
value, not backed by any protocol reserve, and not AXUSD, AXAU,
AXM, SEED, or KAG.

The Sui layer uses a Merkle-based claim architecture with:
  - MAX_PROOF_DEPTH enforcement (depth 20, supporting 1M+ recipients)
  - GuardedTreasury supply cap (1,000,000,000,000,000 units maximum)
  - On-chain proof verification for all claims

13.4 Bridge Governance

Cross-chain asset movements are governed by the Bridge Allowlist —
an on-chain registry of authorized bridge contracts and asset
routes. No asset can bridge through an unauthorized contract.
Allowlist updates require AXM governance votes, preventing any
unauthorized cross-chain exposure.

14.1 Our Approach to Compliance

Compliance is not a legal department problem at Axiom Protocol.
It is an architecture problem, and we solve it at the architecture
level.

ERC-3643 identity enforcement is in the AXUSD contract — not in
a terms-of-service document. Multi-party authorization is in the
BitGo CaaS custody setup — not in a policy memo. Bridge allowlists
are in governance — not in a founder's discretion.

When compliance is built into the structure, it cannot be
accidentally removed, conveniently forgotten under competitive
pressure, or overridden by a bad actor with admin access.

14.2 The Glossary Standard

Axiom Protocol maintains a canonical glossary (lib/glossary.ts)
that defines approved terms, forbidden phrases, and institutional
vocabulary for all allocator-facing communication. This is
enforced across the protocol's interface, documentation, and
content.

  APPROVED INSTITUTIONAL VOCABULARY:
    "automated control layers"       not "smart contracts"
    "multi-party authorization"      not "multi-sig"
    "on-chain financial rails"       not "DeFi"
    "asset onboarding/issuance"      not "tokenization"
    "participation lockup"           not "staking"

Technical terms are available in the protocol glossary at
/disclosure for participants who want the precise definitions.

14.3 Forbidden Positioning

The protocol maintains strict prohibitions on:
  - Absolutist market claims ("only platform," "the standard")
  - Unqualified physical asset claims without evidence
  - Wealth outcome promises of any kind
  - Fixed APY claims (all yield is described as variable)
  - Definitive legal conclusions about token classification
  - GENIUS Act "compliance" claims (only "alignment" language used)

14.4 SEC Reg D 506(c)

The Lending Fund and Syndication Module operate under SEC
Regulation D, Rule 506(c). General solicitation is permitted.
Participation is restricted to verified accredited investors.
No registration statement has been filed with the SEC. This
is not an offer to sell securities in any jurisdiction where
such an offer would be unlawful without registration.

14.5 GENIUS Act Framework

AXUSD is designed to align with the principles of the GENIUS
Act framework for payment stablecoin issuers, including:
  - Reserve backing and transparency requirements
  - Redemption at par value on demand
  - Prohibition on algorithmic backing mechanisms
  - Regulatory reporting principles

"Designed to align with" means the protocol's architecture
reflects these principles. It does not mean the protocol has
received a regulatory determination of compliance.

SMART CONTRACT RISK
Automated control layers may contain bugs, vulnerabilities, or
logic errors. The Sui community layer has deferred external audit.

fully risk-free. Contract exploits can result in permanent loss

REGULATORY RISK
Digital asset regulation is evolving rapidly and unpredictably.
Changes in law, regulatory interpretation, or enforcement action
may affect protocol operations, product availability, token

ORACLE RISK
Asset pricing depends on ERC-7726 and LandNAVOracle. Oracle

LIQUIDITY RISK
AXUSD, AXAU, and AXM may have limited secondary market liquidity.
Participants may be unable to exit positions at expected prices
or within expected timeframes.

REAL ASSET RISK
Real estate values are subject to market cycles, regulatory
changes, environmental conditions, and operator execution.
Land pipeline projections are not guarantees of acquisition,
return, or timeline.

VARIABLE YIELD RISK
All protocol yield rates are variable. No rate is fixed or
guaranteed. Utilization rates, market conditions, and governance
decisions all affect realized yield. Past yield rates are not
indicative of future rates.

CUSTODY RISK
BitGo CaaS provides institutional-grade custody but is not
a guarantee against loss. Participants using self-custody wallets
bear full responsibility for private key management.

COUNTERPARTY RISK
Protocol integrations introduce counterparty dependencies.
Failure, insolvency, or regulatory action affecting Aave,
Euler, Camelot, Coinbase, Stripe, BitGo, or DeNet could
affect protocol operations.

GOVERNANCE RISK
Governance decisions made by AXM holders may not align with
the interests of all participants. Governance attacks, voter
apathy, or capture by a concentrated holder group could result
in adverse protocol changes.
