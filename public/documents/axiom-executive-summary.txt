AXUSD — SETTLEMENT STABLECOIN
ERC-3643 compliant. Automated KYC enforced at the contract level.
A Parity Stability Module keeps the peg honest. Integrated with
Euler V2 lending markets for variable yield. Designed to align
with the GENIUS Act framework. This is what institutional-grade
stablecoin infrastructure looks like when compliance is built in
from the ground up — not bolted on later.

AXAU — GOLD RESERVE INSTRUMENT
Backed by PAXG. Settled on Arbitrum One. Held in your wallet,
not a bank vault. Enter with Stripe or Coinbase. Redemption
returns PAXG — not a dollar promise — so your gold stays
programmable. Priced through our ERC-7726 oracle and the
LandNAVOracle. Gold has never worked like this before.

AXM — GOVERNANCE TOKEN
The AXM token is how decisions get made. Treasury policy.
Asset onboarding. Protocol parameters. Staking schedules.
Bridge allowlists. All of it governed on-chain by AXM holders.
No admin override. No hidden multisig. Governance is not a
feature of this protocol — it is the protocol.

THE TREASURY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Our live, multi-strategy treasury vault is deployed on Arbitrum One.
Capital is routed across Aave V3 and Camelot liquidity strategies
through the StrategyManager. Every rebalance is gated through
Axiom Sentinel — our capital risk authorization engine that issues
time-bound, non-transferable authorization tokens before a single
dollar moves on-chain.

The MIRDT Capital Intelligence Terminal provides macro-regime
detection and AI-assisted allocation signals. BitGo CaaS
institutional custody secures protocol assets with multi-party
authorization. Every event is recorded. Every action is auditable.

This is not an experiment. The treasury is live and operational.

THE LAND PIPELINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Land is generational wealth. Axiom Protocol is building the
framework to bring it on-chain.

Our pipeline identifies distressed real estate through data-driven
analysis, underwrites it through the IVCEE engine, captures field
data through Layer 5 field operations, and issues AxiomLandReceipt
NFTs on Arbitrum One as on-chain proof of ownership.

Every acquisition is governance-authorized. Every property lifecycle
— acquisition, rehabilitation, disposition — is recorded and auditable.
The AI Acquisition Memo Builder generates structured investment
summaries. RentCast and Walk Score data inform underwriting.

From physical land to on-chain receipt. End to end.

THE WEALTH PRACTICE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

For centuries, communities have pooled wealth through rotating
credit circles — susu in West Africa, partner in the Caribbean,
hui in East Asia, tanda in Latin America. These systems work
because they are governed by trust and community accountability,
not credit scores and collateral requirements.

The Wealth Practice brings this model on-chain. Members form
groups, rotate pooled contributions, and build on-chain credit
history that connects directly to protocol lending facilities
and governance participation.

This is not a crypto twist on an old idea. It is the formalization
of proven community wealth mechanics on infrastructure that scales.
