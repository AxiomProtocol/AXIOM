2. BUSINESS DESCRIPTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Axiom Nexus LLC is a financial technology and blockchain software infrastructure
company developing compliance-focused digital asset systems, smart contract tools,
tokenized real-world asset infrastructure, treasury management technology, and
payment rail integrations.


3. NATURE OF BUSINESS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Axiom Nexus LLC develops software infrastructure for digital finance, tokenized
real-world assets, on-chain treasury management, smart contract systems, compliance
tooling, payment rail integrations, dashboards, and related fintech platform
technology.

Primary Technology and Service Areas:

  • Blockchain infrastructure and smart contract development
  • Compliance-native digital asset systems
  • Treasury management technology
  • Tokenized real-world asset platform infrastructure
  • Payment rail integration tooling
  • Business dashboards and reporting systems
  • Fintech platform operations
  • On-chain identity and governance systems


4. CURRENT BUSINESS STAGE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Axiom Nexus LLC is currently an early-stage software and technology company. The
company is primarily engaged in software development, platform infrastructure,
compliance preparation, technical architecture, vendor setup, and business
operations. No regulated financial products are currently offered to the public.


5. ACCOUNT USAGE STATEMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Business accounts will be used for internal company treasury activity,
owner-funded operating expenses, software development costs, infrastructure
expenses, vendor payments, compliance preparation, and general business
operations.


6. SOURCE OF FUNDS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The company is currently funded by owner's capital contributed by the
founder/member for formation, software development, infrastructure, compliance
preparation, and operating expenses. Funds are not derived from customer
deposits, customer custody, money transmission activity, public investment
offerings, or third-party client funds.


7. COMPLIANCE CLARIFICATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Axiom Nexus LLC does not currently:

  • Custody customer funds through this account
  • Transmit customer funds through this account
  • Operate as a bank or licensed money transmitter
  • Provide investment advisory services
  • Operate a virtual currency brokerage or OTC trading desk
  • Hold client escrow funds

Any regulated activity, if launched in the future, will be conducted only through
applicable legal review, licensed third-party providers, approved compliance
procedures, and required registrations or exemptions.
