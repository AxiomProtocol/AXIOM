23. 6 MANDATORY GUARD RAILS
========================================================================

These guard rails are non-negotiable operational constraints:

Guard Rail 1 — Fee Recipient Assumption Check
  Before any fee receiver configuration, verify vault fees are
  non-zero on-chain. Never assume fee routing is active without
  explicit on-chain confirmation.

Guard Rail 2 — Revenue Router Accounting Visibility
  Never trust balance assumptions. Always perform explicit balance
  read plus event verification on the Revenue Router before
  reporting any revenue figures.

Guard Rail 3 — ERC4626 Share Math Edge Case
  On every vault deposit, assert minimum shares output is greater
  than zero. The first depositor received 1:1 shares — this must
  be verified for subsequent deposits as well.

Guard Rail 4 — Self-Borrow Risk Contamination
  All founder loopback test positions must be tagged as
  NON-REPRESENTATIVE in all reporting. These positions exist for
  contract validation, not as evidence of organic demand.

Guard Rail 5 — Sentinel Authority Boundary
  Sentinel is ADVISORY ONLY until a post-public governance vote
  explicitly grants execution authority. No automated trades are
  permitted. Every Sentinel output is informational only.

Guard Rail 6 — Property Phase Timing Risk
  If no qualifying property is identified by Week 44 of the
  playbook, a mandatory HARD PAUSE is executed on the property
  acquisition track. This prevents capital deployment into
  suboptimal real estate opportunities.
