## 1. What Is AXAU?

AXAU is a reserve unit issued on Arbitrum One by Axiom Protocol. It is designed to hold reserve-grade value backed by gold and real property assets. Unlike synthetic gold or commodity ETFs, AXAU is issued through an identity-verified process, held in a self-custody compatible wallet, and backed by auditable on-chain reserves.

AXAU is not a speculative instrument. It is designed for participants who want reserve-grade asset exposure without relying on a custodian, a brokerage, or geographic intermediaries.

The unit is issued under the ERC-3643 identity-gated standard, meaning every holder must pass identity verification before their wallet can hold or transfer AXAU. This is a deliberate design choice that enables regulatory alignment and allows Axiom to maintain a clean, verified participant registry.

---

## 2. Reserve Backing

AXAU is backed by two reserve sources:

**PAXG (Pax Gold)**  
Pax Gold is an ERC-20 token where each unit is backed by one troy ounce of London Good Delivery gold held in Brink's vaults. PAXG is redeemable for physical gold and is issued by Paxos Trust Company. The backing is publicly verifiable on-chain. AXAU uses PAXG as its primary gold reserve layer.

**LandNAV Oracle**  
A verified on-chain oracle that prices real property net asset value. This supplements the gold reserve with real estate valuation, providing a diversified underlying reserve calculation. The oracle is part of Axiom's broader physical-digital bridge infrastructure connecting land acquisition pipelines to on-chain financial rails.

Together, these two reserves are auditable without quarterly disclosures, third-party attestations, or trust-based reporting. Any participant can verify the backing directly on-chain.

## 7. Infrastructure Details (Non-Technical Summary)

AXAU operates on Arbitrum One, a layer-2 network secured by Ethereum. It is compatible with standard Ethereum wallets.

The identity system uses ERC-3643, a standard that gates wallet permissions through verified identity claims. This means the automated control layers enforce participant eligibility without manual intervention at each transaction.

The PSM (Peg Stability Module) connects AXUSD (Axiom's unified stablecoin) and AXAU, creating a pathway for participants who want to move between stablecoin positions and the reserve unit through Axiom's on-chain financial rails.

The reserve unit is NOT a stablecoin. It does not maintain a peg to a fiat currency. Its value is derived from the underlying PAXG and LandNAV reserves.

## 12. FAQ Source Content

**Q: What is AXAU backed by?**  
AXAU is backed by PAXG (Pax Gold, redeemable for physical gold) and a LandNAV Oracle that prices verified real property on-chain. Both reserves are auditable on Arbitrum One.

**Q: Is this a stablecoin?**  
No. AXAU does not maintain a peg to a fiat currency. Its value is derived from its underlying reserves, not a peg mechanism.

**Q: Why do I need to verify my identity?**  
AXAU is issued under an ERC-3643 identity-gated standard. Identity verification is required by the automated control layers governing who can hold and transfer the reserve unit. This enables regulatory alignment and ensures a clean participant registry.

**Q: How many slots are available?**  
100 founding access slots. The program closes when all 100 are filled. There is no secondary waitlist.

**Q: What wallet do I need?**  
Any Ethereum-compatible wallet address (starting with 0x) that you control. MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, and similar wallets are compatible.

**Q: What countries are accepted?**  
Approved countries include the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, France, Netherlands, Switzerland, Singapore, UAE, Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, Bahamas, Bermuda, Brazil, Mexico, Japan, South Korea, and India, among others.

**Q: Is there a minimum investment?**  
The early access application has no minimum investment requirement. Slot approval is based on identity verification, not capital size.

**Q: How is this different from buying a gold ETF?**  
A gold ETF is held by a brokerage on your behalf, audited by a third party, and settled with traditional market infrastructure. AXAU is held in your own wallet, backed by on-chain reserves that are auditable in real time, with no broker intermediary.
