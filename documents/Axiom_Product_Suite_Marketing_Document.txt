I. INTRODUCTION

Axiom Protocol is governance-first wealth infrastructure focused on real
asset acquisition through community capital formation. The platform
provides the tools, data, and on-chain financial rails that allow
everyday people to participate in institutional-grade real estate
investment, from initial property discovery through community-governed
land ownership.

This document details the eight core products that form the Axiom
ecosystem. Each product serves a distinct function, but together they
create a connected pipeline that moves participants from education and
analysis through capital formation and physical asset acquisition.

Every tool in this suite is built on three principles:

1. Real Data Only. No placeholder numbers. No simulated listings.
   Every property, every metric, every photo comes from verified
   government sources, on-chain records, or auditable third-party
   data providers.

2. Disclosure-First Transparency. Every estimate includes confidence
   scores. Every rate is described as variable. Every risk flag is
   surfaced, not hidden. Participants make informed decisions with
   full visibility into how numbers are calculated.

3. Community Governance. Capital moves through multi-party
   authorization. Land acquisitions require community votes. The
   protocol operates through automated control layers on Arbitrum
   One, not through a single centralized authority.

II. PROPERTY ANALYSIS

Route: /property
Status: Live
Pricing: Free tier available. Base ($4.99) and Premium ($14.99)
tiers expanding.

The Property Analysis tool provides structured, data-driven reports
for any US residential property. Enter an address and receive a
comprehensive analysis built from multiple verified data sources.

WHAT YOU GET

A Property Analysis report gives you the critical numbers before
you commit time or money to a deal:

  Value Range: Probabilistic estimates with low, mid, and high
  scenarios. Not a single number pretending to be precise. You see
  the range because real estate valuation is never exact.

  Rent Estimates: Monthly rent projections based on comparable
  properties in the area, giving you the income side of any
  investment calculation.

  Rehab Cost Estimates: Line-item rehabilitation cost projections
  with cost bands so you can budget for improvements before you
  close.

  Deal Grade: A letter grade (A through F) that synthesizes
  valuation, income potential, and risk factors into a single
  quality indicator.

  Confidence Score: A 0-100 rating that tells you how much data
  backs up the analysis. High confidence means multiple data points
  agree. Low confidence means you need more due diligence.

  Risk Flags: Specific concerns surfaced automatically, each with
  a severity level, so you know exactly where to focus your
  attention before making an offer.

  Neighborhood Context: Census demographics, walkability scores,
  and transit access for the surrounding area.

DATA SOURCES

Property Analysis draws from Census Bureau data, the Federal Housing
Finance Agency (FHFA), OpenStreetMap, RentCast, and additional
proprietary and public datasets. Every source is attributed in the
report so you can verify the inputs independently.

ANALYSIS TIERS

  Free Tier: Value and rent ranges, rehab cost bands, confidence
  score, risk flags, and Census-based neighborhood context. No
  account required.

  Base Tier: Everything in Free, plus tighter valuation ranges,
  detailed property specifications, tax assessment history, sale
  history, and the Deal Grade.

  Premium Tier: Everything in Base, plus rental comparables,
  Walk Score and Transit Score integration, and full neighborhood
  demographic analysis.

WHY IT MATTERS

Most property analysis tools give you one number and call it a value.
That is not how real estate works. Every property sits within a range
of possible values depending on condition, timing, and comparable
sales. The Axiom Property Analysis tool gives you that range, tells
you how confident it is, and flags specific risks so you walk into
every conversation with your eyes open.

III. DEAL INTELLIGENCE

Route: /deal-intelligence
Status: Live

Deal Intelligence is the institutional-grade underwriting engine at
the center of the Axiom platform. It takes raw property data and
transforms it into a complete investment analysis with financial
modeling, risk assessment, AI-powered advisory, and decision
documentation.

PROPERTY SEARCH AND DATA ENRICHMENT

Start with any address. The system normalizes it, searches existing
records, and if it is new, creates a full property profile enriched
with data from verified providers. You get square footage, bedroom
and bathroom counts, year built, zoning classification, tax history,
sale history, lot size, and geographic coordinates, all populated
automatically.

STRATEGY SELECTION

Choose your investment approach and the underwriting engine adapts:

  BRRRR (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat): Optimized for
  investors planning to force appreciation through renovation and
  then refinance to recapture capital.

  Fix and Flip: Focused on purchase price, rehab budget, and ARV
  (After Repair Value) to calculate total return on a short-term
  renovation project.

  Buy and Hold: Long-term rental analysis with emphasis on monthly
  cash flow, DSCR, and cap rate sustainability.

  Note: For investors purchasing mortgage notes rather than
  physical property.

  Multifamily: Underwriting for 2-4 unit and larger residential
  income properties.

UNDERWRITING AND FINANCIAL MODELING

The Deal Workspace lets you build detailed financial scenarios with
granular control over every assumption:

  Acquisition: Purchase price, ARV, closing costs, and rehab budget.
  Financing: Down payment percentage, interest rate, loan term, and
  points.
  Income: Monthly rent, vacancy rate, and other income sources.
  Expenses: Property management, maintenance, insurance, property
  taxes, and capital expenditure reserves.

From these inputs, the system computes:

  Net Operating Income (NOI): Annual rental income minus operating
  expenses.
  Cap Rate: NOI divided by purchase price, the fundamental measure
  of property yield.
  Cash-on-Cash Return: Annual cash flow divided by total cash
  invested.
  DSCR: Debt Service Coverage Ratio, your rental income relative
  to your loan payment. Lenders require this above 1.25.
  Monthly and Annual Cash Flow: The actual money in your pocket
  after all expenses and debt service.
  Break-Even Analysis: How many months until the property pays back
  your initial investment.

You can create multiple scenarios (optimistic, base case, stress
case) and compare them side by side.

COMPARABLE SALES

The system pulls recent nearby sales to validate your ARV assumptions
and market positioning. You see what similar properties actually sold
for, not what someone listed them at.

IVCEE: INSTITUTIONAL VIABILITY AND CAPITAL EFFICIENCY ENGINE

IVCEE is the allocator-grade risk engine that separates Axiom from
basic property calculators. It provides six layers of institutional
analysis:

  Probability Model: Calculates viability probability versus failure
  probability and identifies the dominant risk factor threatening
  the deal.

  Stress Tests: Projects whether the deal survives under adverse
  conditions including DSCR compression, cash flow drawdowns,
  vacancy spikes, and interest rate increases.

  Sensitivity Matrix: Shows exactly how changes in purchase price,
  rent levels, or interest rates shift the deal from viable to
  marginal to unviable.

  Refinance Risk: Models loan-to-value, DSCR at refinance, and
  equity extraction probability at the end of your hold period.

  Downside Protection: Calculates break-even rent, break-even
  purchase price, and your margin of safety on the deal.

  Capital Efficiency: Scores how efficiently your capital is
  deployed and ranks this deal against every other deal in the
  system.

AI ACQUISITION ADVISORY

After underwriting is complete, the AI advisor analyzes all metrics
and delivers:

  Verdict: A clear recommendation (Strong Proceed, Conditional
  Proceed, Hold, or Reject) with a confidence score.

  Offer Strategy: Recommended maximum offer price, walk-away price,
  and specific negotiation leverage points based on the property
  data.

  Creative Structures: Alternative acquisition methods (creative
  financing, seller concessions, assignment) with associated risk
  levels for each approach.

  Risk Management Plan: Specific reserve requirements, contingency
  provisions, and exit scenarios if conditions change.

  Path to Viability: For marginal deals, step-by-step improvements
  that could make the numbers work.

DECISION LOG

Every decision on every deal is formally recorded with the rationale,
the metrics at the time of decision, and the outcome. This creates an
auditable track record of your investment discipline.

IV. DEAL FLOW

Route: /distressed-feed
Status: Live
Current Listings: 282 active properties across 8 states

Deal Flow is the distressed property acquisition pipeline that
aggregates real foreclosure listings from government agencies into
a single searchable feed with real photos, real prices, and direct
links to institutional-grade analysis.

REAL GOVERNMENT DATA

Every listing in the Deal Flow feed comes from verified government
sources. The primary data source is the U.S. Department of Housing
and Urban Development (HUD), which lists foreclosed properties
through its official HUD HomeStore platform. These are real
properties with real addresses, real asking prices, and real
photographs hosted on government infrastructure.

The system currently tracks 282 active listings across 8 southeastern
states: Texas (144), Florida (32), Alabama (26), Georgia (24),
North Carolina (21), Mississippi (15), Tennessee (13), and
South Carolina (7).

Additional government sources (Fannie Mae HomePath, Freddie Mac
HomeSteps, USDA Rural Development) are configured and attempt
automated data collection. These agencies use varying levels of
access protection, and the system reports transparently when data
retrieval is blocked rather than substituting placeholder information.

WHAT YOU SEE

Each listing includes:

  Property photos from the original government listing (real
  Cloudinary-hosted images, not stock photos or AI-generated
  images).

  Address, city, state, zip code, and county.

  List price set by the government agency.

  Property specifications: bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage,
  year built, and property type.

  Distress type classification: government foreclosure, REO,
  tax lien, wholesale, short sale, or auction.

  Direct link to source listing on the government website.

FILTERS

Narrow the feed by state, city, distress type, property type,
minimum and maximum price, and sort by newest, price, discount
percentage, or auction date.

BUY BOX MATCHING

Set your acquisition criteria once and the system matches incoming
deals automatically:

  Target states and cities.
  Price range.
  Property types and distress types.
  Minimum bedrooms and square footage.
  Maximum price per square foot.

When new listings match your buy box, they are flagged and queued
for your review.

WHOLESALER SUBMISSION PORTAL

Licensed wholesalers can submit off-market deals directly into the
pipeline. Submissions include property details, asking price, ARV,
rehab estimate, and contract end date. Every submission is reviewed
within 48 hours before being published to the community feed.

INSTANT DEAL ANALYSIS

Every listing includes an "Analyze Deal" button that sends the
property directly to Deal Intelligence. One click populates the
address, resolves the property data, enriches it with verified
sources, and opens a full underwriting workspace. No copy-pasting
addresses. No manual data entry. From discovery to institutional-
grade analysis in seconds.

V. CAPITAL PROGRAM

Route: /capital
Status: Live

The Capital Accounting and Performance Intelligence System is the
institutional-grade financial backbone of the Axiom ecosystem. It
provides double-entry accounting, position tracking, and performance
analytics for all capital deployed through the protocol.

DOUBLE-ENTRY LEDGER

Every capital event is recorded as a formal double-entry transaction
with debit and credit amounts, account classifications (Asset,
Liability, Equity, Revenue, Expense), and sub-type categorization
(Cash, Trading, Fee Reserve, Operating). Every entry includes a
transaction group ID and source attribution for complete auditability.

POSITION TRACKING

The system tracks investment positions from open to close, recording
instrument, venue, strategy, quantity, average entry and exit prices,
and realized profit and loss. Trade executions from the MIRDT paper
trading system feed directly into formal capital accounting, bridging
experimental analysis with institutional record-keeping.

IMMUTABLE SNAPSHOTS

Point-in-time snapshots capture the complete state of the capital
ecosystem. Each snapshot includes:

  A cryptographic checksum to verify data integrity.

  A confidence rating (High or Medium) based on the availability
  and freshness of price marks.

  External context including regime assessments from Axiom Sentinel
  and policy states from the Graduated Execution Framework.

  Capital drift analysis comparing expected balances (from ledger
  entries) against realized values (from actual trades and fees)
  to identify accounting variances.

PERFORMANCE INTELLIGENCE

The system calculates advanced metrics across multiple timeframes
(daily, weekly, monthly, annual, and all-time):

  Realized and unrealized P/L with mark-to-market valuation.

  Return on Capital (RoC) and Return on Deployed Capital (RoDC).

  Capital Efficiency Score combining deployment utilization with
  return metrics.

  Variance Stability Index (VSI) measuring the consistency of
  returns over time.

  Drawdown Analysis tracking peak-to-trough declines, depth
  percentages, and recovery durations.

VI. LENDING FUND

Route: /lending-fund
Status: Configured

The Axiom Lending Fund provides property-secured bridge capital for
real estate acquisition and development. It is structured as an SEC
Regulation D 506(c) offering and operates with on-chain settlement
on Arbitrum One.

FUND STRUCTURE

The fund provides short-term bridge loans (up to 12 months) for
Fix and Flip and property acquisition strategies. All loans are
secured by real property at a maximum 70% loan-to-value ratio on
the after-repair value. Interest rates are variable and determined
by risk tier assessment.

ON-CHAIN SETTLEMENT

Fund operations including loan originations, interest collections,
and repayments are recorded on Arbitrum One for full auditability.
The fund uses ERC-4626 compatible vaults to manage assets and issue
proportional shares to participants.

INVESTOR ONBOARDING

Participation in the Lending Fund follows a structured compliance
process:

  Wallet Authentication: Web3 signature verification linking wallet
  addresses to legal profiles.

  Compliance Acknowledgment: Review and acceptance of the Private
  Placement Memorandum (PPM) and Risk Disclosure Supplement.

  Accredited Investor Verification: Questionnaire-based verification
  of accredited status as required by SEC Rule 506(c). Participants
  must meet net worth or income thresholds established by the SEC.

  Subscription: On-chain signing of subscription agreements
  committing AXUSD to the fund vault.

LOAN APPLICATIONS

Borrowers submit detailed applications including property address,
purchase price, rehab budget, and estimated ARV. The underwriting
engine calculates DSCR, LTV, and assigns a risk tier. Applications
are reviewed, approved, and funded through the protocol's
administrative dashboard.

RISK MANAGEMENT

  Maximum 70% LTV on after-repair value limits exposure on every
  individual loan.

  DSCR requirements ensure the property's income supports debt
  service. Applications with DSCR below 1.25 are flagged for
  additional review.

  Loan performance, delinquency tracking, and collection activity
  are recorded on-chain for participant visibility.

VII. UNIFIED AXUSD (ERC-3643)

Route: /axusd-3643
Status: Live (13 verified contracts on Arbitrum One)

AXUSD is the protocol stablecoin rebuilt on the ERC-3643 (T-REX)
standard, the institutional framework for compliant real-world
asset issuance. It replaces the previous dual-ecosystem
architecture with a single, unified token that embeds identity
verification and compliance enforcement directly on-chain.

ON-CHAIN IDENTITY (ONCHAINID)

Every AXUSD holder has an on-chain identity contract built on
EIP-734/735 standards. This identity stores verified claims
(KYC Verified, Accredited Investor, Sanctions Clear) issued by
authorized claim issuers. Identity verification happens once and
applies across all protocol interactions, no repeated KYC for
every transaction.

MODULAR COMPLIANCE

Compliance is not hard-coded into the token. It is managed through
four upgradeable on-chain modules:

  CountryAllowModule: Restricts transfers to approved jurisdictions.

  MaxBalanceModule: Enforces per-holder balance caps to prevent
  concentration risk.

  TransferLimitModule: Rate-limits transfer volume over defined
  time periods to detect and prevent wash trading or suspicious
  activity.

  LendingPlatformModule: Whitelists verified lending and liquidity
  platforms for compliant participation in on-chain financial rails.

Because compliance is modular, the protocol can adapt to changing
regulatory requirements without redeploying the token contract.

DESIGNED TO ALIGN WITH THE GENIUS ACT

The ERC-3643 architecture is designed to align with emerging
stablecoin legislation including the GENIUS Act. The modular
compliance framework, on-chain identity infrastructure, and
automated control layers provide the technical foundation for
regulatory alignment as the legislative landscape evolves.

DASHBOARD

The AXUSD dashboard provides four views:

  Overview: Token supply, network status, registered identities,
  compliance modules, and whitelisted platforms.

  Identity: Your personal ONCHAINID contract, verification tier,
  country code, and verified claims.

  Compliance: Active module details, pre-flight transfer checks
  (verify a transfer will pass before submitting), and compliance
  activity log.

  Contracts: Full deployment registry of all 13 verified contracts
  with Arbiscan links and architecture documentation.

VIII. THE WEALTH PRACTICE

Route: /wealth-practice
Status: Staged Rollout

The Wealth Practice is a structured group savings framework with
deterministic scheduling, participant-level transparency, and
cryptographic audit trails. It modernizes the traditional community
savings model with on-chain verification and formal documentation.

HOW IT WORKS

A group of participants agrees to contribute a fixed amount on a
regular schedule. Each cycle, the total pool is allocated to one
participant until everyone in the group has received their
allocation. The result is accelerated access to capital through
collective discipline rather than individual debt.

Unlike informal savings arrangements, The Wealth Practice provides:

  On-chain transparency through automated control layers on
  Arbitrum One. Every contribution and allocation is recorded,
  timestamped, and verifiable.

  Documented savings history that transforms informal community
  saving into a formal financial record. This documentation can
  support mortgage applications and other lending relationships
  where proof of consistent savings discipline is required.

  Multi-party authorization ensuring funds move according to the
  agreed schedule, not at the discretion of any single individual.

THE THREE-STAGE TRUST PIPELINE

The Wealth Practice uses a structured onboarding process designed
to build trust before capital is committed:

  Stage 1 - Interest Hub (Discovery): Participants join city-based
  or goal-based hubs to connect with others who share similar
  financial objectives. No financial commitment required. This is
  where you find your people.

  Stage 2 - Purpose Group (Commitment): Participants form a smaller,
  committed group with defined goals, contribution amounts,
  schedules, and agreed-upon rules. Everyone knows the terms before
  any money moves.

  Stage 3 - On-Chain Pool (Execution): The savings practice goes
  live on Arbitrum One with automated rotations and cryptographic
  audit trails. Contributions and allocations execute according to
  the agreed schedule through automated control layers.

CAPITAL FLOW BRIDGE

The Wealth Practice connects directly to the physical asset pipeline.
Capital accumulated through disciplined group savings can flow into:

  Land acquisition pools, using participation credits to contribute
  toward community land purchases.

  Housing pathways, connecting saved capital to rent-to-own programs
  and down payment assistance.

  Documentation for traditional lenders, providing formal proof of
  savings discipline that banks recognize when evaluating mortgage
  applications.

The practice is not an investment product. It does not promise or
imply any return. It is a framework for disciplined capital formation
within community-governed parameters, designed to support financial
resilience and expand structural wealth-building capacity.

IX. LAND

Route: /land
Status: Planned

The Land product represents the physical-digital bridge of the Axiom
Protocol. It is a community-governed framework for identifying,
evaluating, funding, acquiring, and activating physical land through
collective capital and transparent governance.

THE ACQUISITION PIPELINE

Every land acquisition moves through a structured lifecycle:

  Candidate: A property is submitted for community consideration
  with physical attributes (acreage, location, property type),
  financial data (asking price, estimated value), and a proposed
  stewardship plan.

  Due Diligence: Rigorous verification including title review,
  survey verification, mineral rights assessment, and environmental
  screening. Each checkpoint is documented and visible to the
  community.

  Community Vote: The acquisition requires a formal governance
  proposal where participants vote for or against. Proposals must
  meet a defined quorum to be valid. No land is purchased without
  community authorization.

  Funding: A purpose pool is created to raise the required capital
  in AXUSD. Participants contribute according to their capacity
  and receive proportional participation credits.

  Acquired: Legal purchase and transfer into the community treasury
  structure.

  Activated: The land is placed into production or development
  under the management of certified stewards.

COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE

Every significant decision in the land lifecycle requires community
participation:

  Acquisition proposals with full financial disclosure and
  stewardship plans.

  Voting with quorum requirements to prevent decisions by small
  minorities.

  Steward appointments approved through governance process.

  Ongoing operational decisions managed through the protocol's
  multi-party authorization framework.

STEWARD CORPS

The Steward Corps training program prepares participants to manage
physical assets. Training tiers (Premium, Standard, and Scholarship)
provide education in land management, regenerative agriculture,
and community development. Certified stewards are eligible to be
appointed as managers of acquired land parcels.

PARTICIPATION AND PRODUCE

Once land is activated, the community benefits directly:

  Produce Reservations: Participants can reserve produce boxes from
  community agricultural operations, connecting digital capital
  participation to physical food access.

  Participation Credits: Earned through consistent engagement with
  The Wealth Practice and other protocol activities, these credits
  can be applied toward produce, housing pathways, and future land
  pool contributions.

  Fractional Participation: Through purpose pools and on-chain
  asset onboarding, participants can hold proportional interests
  in physical land that would be inaccessible to individual buyers.
