AXAG is **not live**, **not approved for deployment**, **not minted**, and **not listed**. ## How to use these templates

1. Replace all `[BRACKETED FIELDS]` with the appropriate value before sending.
2. Templates are formatted as plain text for copy-paste into any email client.
3. Attach `AXAG_CUSTODY_RFP.md` (or a PDF export of it) to Template 1.
4. Templates 2 and 3 assume Template 1 has already been sent to the recipient.
5. Send all communications from a named Axiom Protocol email address — not a personal address.
6. Log all outreach in the tracker's C-02 notes field with the date and recipient.
7. All responses received must be filed in the C-02 evidence folder and logged.

## Template 2 — Follow-Up Questions Email

To be sent if a custodian has responded to Template 1 but their response requires clarification, or if they have confirmed interest but need additional context before providing a formal response.

Subject line: AXAG Custody Diligence — Follow-Up Questions for [CUSTODIAN NAME]

---

```text
To: [RECIPIENT NAME], [RECIPIENT TITLE]
Organization: [CUSTODIAN ENTITY NAME]
From: [AXIOM SENDER NAME], [AXIOM SENDER TITLE], Axiom Protocol
Date: [DATE]
Subject: AXAG Custody Diligence — Follow-Up Questions for [CUSTODIAN NAME]
Reference: Our inquiry of [DATE OF TEMPLATE 1 EMAIL]

Dear [RECIPIENT NAME],

Thank you for [confirming receipt of our inquiry / your initial response dated [DATE]]. We appreciate your engagement.

Following our initial outreach (and your response, where provided), we have a series of follow-up questions that will help us evaluate your organization's fit for the AXAG custody requirement. These questions are drawn from the evaluation criteria in the RFP document previously shared.

Please respond to the questions below at your convenience. If any question falls outside your area of responsibility, please direct it to the appropriate colleague and copy us on the response.

---

Section A — Physical silver and vault operations

A1. Confirm whether your organization holds physical silver in LBMA Good Delivery form (minimum .999 fineness, 750–1,100 troy ounce bars). If yes, confirm the vault location(s) and whether they appear on the LBMA Good Delivery List.

A2. Describe how client assets are identified in your inventory system. Specifically, are client bars tracked by individual bar serial number, refiner name, assay certificate number, and weight? Is this data accessible to the client on demand or on a scheduled reporting basis?

A3. Describe your maximum storage capacity for silver (in troy ounces or metric tons) at each relevant vault location. Is capacity currently constrained, and what is the minimum account size you would require for a new institutional silver custody client?

A4. Describe the chain-of-custody process for newly acquired silver entering your vault: from refiner to transport carrier to vault receipt. What documentation accompanies the silver at each step?

---

Section B — Segregation and insolvency remoteness

B1. Confirm whether client silver held in allocated accounts is legally segregated from your organization's own assets. Specifically: (a) are client bars individually identified rather than pooled, (b) are client bars off your organization's balance sheet, and (c) in the event of your organization's insolvency, would client bars be available to your general creditors?

B2. What is the legal mechanism by which client assets are segregated from your organization's estate? Is the arrangement structured as a trust, a bailee arrangement, or another legal form? Under which jurisdiction's law?

B3. Has your organization's allocated custody arrangement been reviewed by outside legal counsel for insolvency remoteness? Would you be willing to share a summary of that analysis (redacted for client confidentiality) as part of your RFP response?

---

Section C — Attestation and audit

C1. Describe your organization's existing attestation or audit program for precious metals clients. Who is the appointed auditor? How frequently are attestations performed? What does the attestation scope cover (physical count, serial number reconciliation, or both)?

C2. If your organization does not currently have a monthly attestation program, are you willing to commit to one for a new client? Would Axiom Protocol be permitted to appoint the independent auditor (subject to your reasonable approval), or must you use your own appointed auditor?

C3. Are you willing to grant unannounced inspection rights to an independent inspector appointed by Axiom Protocol, with reasonable credential requirements? What is the minimum notice period you would require?

C4. What is the format of your attestation reports? Are they signed by the auditor and available in a machine-readable format (PDF, structured data file, or API output)?

---

Section D — Redemption

D1. Describe the process for initiating a redemption (return of silver to Axiom Protocol or a designated counterparty). What authentication is required to initiate? Who at your organization receives and processes a redemption instruction?

D2. For each redemption mode (in-vault title transfer, vault-to-vault transfer, physical delivery), what is the achievable settlement window? What is the minimum silver quantity per redemption instruction?

D3. How are sub-bar residuals handled? If a redemption instruction results in a fractional-bar balance, does your organization offer a cash equivalent, an allocated residual account, or another solution?

---

Section E — Technology and digital integration

E1. Does your organization currently have any API, data feed, or digital reporting product that could publish precious metals inventory data in a machine-readable format? If yes, describe the output format, cadence, and whether cryptographic signing is supported.

E2. If no existing product exists, would your organization be willing to develop a machine-readable monthly precious metals inventory report, signed by an authorized representative, for a new institutional client? What is the estimated timeline and cost?

E3. Have you previously integrated with any blockchain-based proof-of-reserves system, oracle, or digital asset platform? If yes, describe the arrangement.

---

Section F — Regulatory and commercial

F1. Confirm the legal entity name, jurisdiction of incorporation, and primary regulatory license(s) held by the entity that would be party to the custody agreement.

F2. Has your organization been subject to any regulatory enforcement action, license revocation, or material legal proceeding in the past five years? If yes, provide a brief summary.

F3. Provide a preliminary fee schedule covering: storage (per troy oz or AUM%), inbound transaction fee, outbound transaction fee, attestation fee (if separately charged), and setup or onboarding fee. Fees should be in USD.

F4. What is the minimum account size (in troy ounces of silver or USD equivalent) required for a new institutional custody relationship?

---
```

## Template 3 — Proof-of-Reserves / Attestation Request Email

To be sent to custodians who have confirmed willingness to proceed and where Axiom Protocol needs to understand the specific attestation workflow and data format before advancing to C-03.

Subject line: AXAG Custody Diligence — Proof-of-Reserves and Attestation Workflow Request

---

```text
To: [RECIPIENT NAME], [RECIPIENT TITLE]
Organization: [CUSTODIAN ENTITY NAME]
From: [AXIOM SENDER NAME], [AXIOM SENDER TITLE], Axiom Protocol
Date: [DATE]
Subject: AXAG Custody Diligence — Proof-of-Reserves and Attestation Workflow Request
Reference: RFP dated [DATE] / Follow-up dated [DATE — if applicable]

Dear [RECIPIENT NAME],

Thank you for your continued engagement with our AXAG custody diligence process. We have reviewed [your initial response / our prior correspondence] and are now seeking additional detail on a specific topic: proof-of-reserves and attestation workflow.

On-chain transparency is a core requirement of our reserve instrument framework. Our reference implementation (AXAU, backed by PAXG / Paxos Gold) publishes monthly attestation data that feeds into an on-chain reporting layer. We are seeking to understand whether and how your organization can support a similar program for physical silver.

The information we are seeking covers three areas:

---

Area 1 — Attestation workflow

Please describe, in as much operational detail as possible, how an attestation would work for an Axiom Protocol silver custody account:

1.1 Who commissions the attestation — Axiom Protocol, your organization, or a jointly appointed auditor?

1.2 What does the attestation scope cover? Please confirm whether the following are included:
    - Physical count of silver bars in the allocated account
    - Bar-serial-number reconciliation against the Axiom Protocol ledger
    - Weight verification of bars (assay certificate match)
    - Insurance coverage confirmation as of the attestation date

1.3 What is the maximum achievable attestation frequency? Can monthly be supported? If not, what is the maximum cadence?

1.4 What is the lead time from engagement to first attestation?

1.5 Who is the auditor you currently use for attestation, or who would you recommend? Is an auditor already engaged with your organization for precious metals clients?

---

Area 2 — Report format and delivery

2.1 What format does the attestation report take? Provide a sample report structure or a template if available (redacted for client confidentiality).

2.2 Is the report signed by the auditor? Is the signature a wet signature, a digital signature (e.g., DocuSign or equivalent), or a cryptographic signature?

2.3 Can the attestation report be delivered in a machine-readable format (structured JSON, XML, or equivalent) in addition to a human-readable PDF?

2.4 Is the report delivered within 30 calendar days of the attestation date? What is the SLA?

---

Area 3 — On-chain integration (if applicable)

3.1 Does your organization have any existing on-chain or blockchain data integration product? If yes, describe it — including the data format, signature mechanism, and any oracle or data availability layer used.

3.2 If no on-chain integration currently exists, are you willing to develop the following for Axiom Protocol's use:
    - A signed JSON payload published to a specified endpoint at a monthly cadence, containing: {date, account_id, silver_bars: [{serial, refiner, weight_troy_oz, assay_cert}], total_troy_oz, auditor_name, auditor_signature}
    - Delivered within 5 business days of each attestation date

3.3 Would your organization be willing to co-sign the attestation data with a digital key registered with Axiom Protocol's oracle layer, so that the on-chain system can verify the authenticity of the data?

3.4 If on-chain integration is not currently possible, would you be willing to provide the signed JSON payload to Axiom Protocol's appointed data intermediary, who would then publish it on-chain? What technical requirements would you have for this arrangement?

---

Area 4 — Unannounced inspection rights

4.1 Confirm whether your organization will agree to unannounced inspection rights for an independent inspector appointed by Axiom Protocol.

4.2 If unannounced inspections are not available, what is the minimum notice period you require?

4.3 What credentials or accreditations must the inspector hold?

4.4 Who bears the cost of unannounced inspections — Axiom Protocol, your organization, or shared?

---

We understand that some of these questions may require internal coordination across operations, legal, and technology teams. We are happy to schedule a working session to walk through these requirements in detail if that would be helpful.

Please respond within [RESPONSE DEADLINE] or contact us to schedule a call.

Axiom Protocol
```
