3. AVAILABLE PRODUCTS OVERVIEW
======================================================================

Product 1: AXM STAKING
  - What it does: Lock AXM tokens to earn variable rewards
  - Variable APY: 12% - 25% (subject to change)
  - Lock-up: None (flexible withdrawal)
  - Minimum: No minimum
  - Income type: Token rewards (compoundable)
  - Risk level: Low-moderate (token price volatility)

Product 2: WEALTH PRACTICE (Group Economics)
  - What it does: Rotating savings circles where members
    contribute weekly/monthly and receive lump-sum payouts
  - Income type: Lump-sum capital accumulation
  - Contribution: Fixed amount agreed by circle members
  - Cycle: Determined by group size (e.g., 10 members =
    10-week cycle)
  - Currency: AXUSD
  - Risk level: Low (insurance fund covers defaults)

Product 3: DEX LIQUIDITY PROVISION
  - What it does: Provide token pairs to Axiom Exchange Hub
    and earn a share of trading fees
  - Fee share: 0.3% of trading volume distributed to LPs
  - Lock-up: None (withdraw anytime)
  - Minimum: No minimum
  - Income type: Trading fees (continuous)
  - Risk level: Moderate (impermanent loss exposure)

Product 4: LENDING FUND PARTICIPATION
  - What it does: Deposit AXUSD into a lending pool that
    funds short-term bridge loans secured by real property
  - Variable target rate: 10% - 14% annually
  - Minimum participation: $100
  - Loan security: Real property (max 70% LTV of ARV)
  - Structure: Designed to align with SEC Reg D 506(c)
  - Income type: Interest distributions
  - Risk level: Low-moderate (property-secured)

Product 5: DePIN NODE INCOME
  - What it does: Operate decentralized infrastructure nodes
    that provide storage, compute, and data services
  - Entry cost: $15 - $40 (smartphone-based starter node)
  - Income type: Service fees and protocol rewards
  - Lock-up: None
  - Risk level: Low (infrastructure service demand)

11. RISK FACTORS & MITIGATION
======================================================================

RISK: AXM token price decline
  Impact: Reduces dollar value of staking position and rewards
  Mitigation: Diversification across AXUSD-denominated products
  (Lending Fund, Wealth Practice) provides stability. Continue
  buying during price dips to lower average cost.

RISK: Smart contract vulnerability
  Impact: Potential loss of deposited funds
  Mitigation: Axiom Protocol uses verified contracts on Arbitrum
  One. Spread capital across multiple products to limit single-
  contract exposure.

RISK: DEX impermanent loss
  Impact: LP position value decreases relative to holding tokens
  Mitigation: Limit DEX allocation to 20% of portfolio. Choose
  stable pairs (AXM/AXUSD) to minimize divergence.

RISK: Lending Fund borrower default
  Impact: Delayed or reduced interest payments
  Mitigation: All loans secured by real property at maximum 70%
  LTV. Default recovery through property liquidation.

RISK: Wealth Practice member default
  Impact: Delayed payout to remaining circle members
  Mitigation: Insurance fund (funded by 5% of DePIN node
  rewards) covers shortfalls. AxiomScore system filters
  unreliable participants.

RISK: AME policy mode change
  Impact: Yield distribution may be reduced or suspended during
  DEFENSIVE, RESTRICTED, or EMERGENCY modes
  Mitigation: This is a protective mechanism. It preserves
  protocol health. Income resumes when stability is restored.
  The hard brake requires 3 consecutive safe snapshots to
  release, preventing premature resumption.

RISK: Regulatory changes
  Impact: Product availability or structure may change
  Mitigation: Axiom Protocol is designed to align with evolving
  regulatory frameworks. Diversification across product types
  reduces single-regulation exposure.

RISK: Missed contributions
  Impact: Delays compounding and extends timeline to target
  Mitigation: Set up a recurring weekly reminder. Even partial
  contributions are better than skipping entirely.

12. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
======================================================================

Q: Can I start with less than $100/week?
A: Yes. The strategy scales linearly. At $50/week, expect the
   same income targets to take approximately 36-48 months
   instead of 24 months. The allocation percentages remain
   the same.

Q: Can I start with more than $100/week?
A: Yes. At $200/week, the timeline compresses significantly.
   The $1,500/month target becomes achievable in approximately
   12-18 months under base case conditions.

Q: What if I miss a week?
A: Resume the following week with the normal $100 allocation.
   Do not try to "catch up" by doubling. Consistency matters
   more than any single contribution.

Q: When should I start withdrawing income?
A: The strategy recommends full reinvestment through Month 18
   and partial withdrawal (25-50%) from Month 19 onward. Early
   withdrawal significantly delays reaching the income target.

Q: What is the minimum portfolio size to generate $1,500/month?
A: At a blended effective yield of 10-15% (achievable through
   combined staking, lending, DEX, and DePIN), you need
   approximately $120,000-$180,000 in deployed capital from
   yield alone. However, the Wealth Practice payout structure
   supplements yield income, reducing the required portfolio
   to approximately $15,000-$25,000 when combined with active
   circle participation.

Q: What happens during an AME hard brake event?
A: Yield distribution is temporarily suspended to protect
   protocol solvency. Your positions remain intact. Income
   resumes after 3 consecutive safe stability snapshots. This
   is a feature, not a failure — it prevents loss of capital.

Q: Is my capital locked?
A: Staking, DEX liquidity, and DePIN nodes have no mandatory
   lock-up. You can withdraw at any time. Wealth Practice
   contributions follow the circle schedule. Lending Fund
   participation follows the fund terms.

Q: What fees should I expect?
A: Arbitrum One gas fees are approximately $0.05-$0.20 per
   transaction. Batch your weekly deployments into a single
   session to minimize total gas costs. There are no platform
   fees on staking or DEX liquidity provision.

Q: Can I track my progress?
A: Each product has a dashboard showing your position, earned
   income, and historical performance. The Solvency page shows
   overall protocol health. The Capital Accounting page provides
   detailed ledger and performance analytics.

13. GLOSSARY OF TERMS
======================================================================

AME (Adaptive Metrics Engine): The deterministic financial
  computation engine that governs protocol policy mode, yield
  distribution, and risk controls.

APY (Annual Percentage Yield): The annualized rate of return
  accounting for compounding. All APY figures in this manual
  are variable and subject to change.

AXUSD: Axiom Protocol's stablecoin, designed to maintain a
  1:1 peg with USD. Used for Wealth Practice contributions,
  Lending Fund participation, and stable-pair DEX liquidity.

AXM: The Axiom Protocol governance token. Used for staking,
  governance voting, DEX liquidity, and protocol participation.

AxiomScore: An on-chain reputation score (300-850) that tracks
  participation reliability. Higher scores qualify for better
  Wealth Practice circle placement and larger capital access.

Compounding: Reinvesting earned income back into the same or
  other products to generate income on the income itself.

DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network): A network
  of physical nodes that provide storage, compute, and data
  services in exchange for protocol rewards.

DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio): Net operating income divided
  by debt service payments. Values above 1.0 indicate the
  ability to cover debt from operating income.

Hard Brake: An automated safety mechanism that suspends yield
  distribution when critical stability thresholds are breached.
  Requires consecutive safe snapshots to release.

Impermanent Loss: The temporary reduction in value of a
  liquidity provider's position compared to simply holding
  the tokens, caused by price divergence between paired tokens.

LP (Liquidity Provider): A user who deposits token pairs into
  a DEX pool to facilitate trading and earn a share of fees.

LTV (Loan-to-Value): The ratio of a loan amount to the value
  of the underlying asset. Lower LTV means more equity cushion.

PSM (Peg Stability Module): The mechanism for minting and
  redeeming AXUSD at a 1:1 ratio with USDC, maintaining the
  stablecoin peg.

Stability Score: A composite score (0-100) measuring protocol
  health across coverage, reserves, liquidity, redemption stress,
  and volatility pressure. Scores above 75 indicate STABLE band.

Wealth Practice: The Axiom Protocol group economics product
