## 1. Overview

Axiom Protocol is governance-first wealth infrastructure for real asset acquisition through community capital formation. The platform provides tools, data, and on-chain financial infrastructure for institutional-grade real estate participation — from initial property discovery through community-governed land ownership. It runs on Arbitrum One, supported by 72 verified automated control layers auditable at arbiscan.io.

Most community wealth-building groups fail not because of bad intentions but because they lack the infrastructure to succeed. Axiom replaces informal spreadsheets and group chats with programmable, auditable financial infrastructure built on three principles:

Transparency — every capital movement, governance decision, and operational action is recorded on-chain. Verifiable records replace informal trust.

Coordination — structure and shared rules create reliable collaboration. Defined roles and accountability loops replace ad-hoc decision making.

Discipline — fixed treasury allocation policies and institutional-grade reporting. Execution over narrative.

Axiom Protocol is not a bank, broker-dealer, or registered investment advisor. Participation requires independent evaluation of all disclosures at `/disclosure`.

## 2. Who This Is For

Axiom Protocol was designed for Black and Latino W-2 earners aged 25-40 in Atlanta, Houston, and Charlotte who are ready to build real wealth through disciplined participation in real estate — and have historically been excluded from institutional-grade capital structures.

The platform also serves real estate professionals building deal pipelines, accredited investors seeking structured real asset exposure, syndicators managing LP offerings, institutional allocators evaluating the protocol, and community organizers building shared capital infrastructure.

## 6. Community

### The Wealth Practice

Route: `/wealth-practice` — Status: Staged Rollout

A structured group savings and allocation program with governance controls and audit trails. Members form groups, make regular contributions, and receive rotating capital access through three stages: Forming (group organizing), Active (contributions collected, rotations executed), and Graduated (full cycle complete — unlocks Capital Program access).

To join or start a group:
1. Navigate to `/wealth-practice` and connect your wallet.
2. Browse existing groups or click New Group to define contribution amount, cycle length, and rotation method.

4. Once the group activates, contributions begin on schedule.

Not an investment product. Not FDIC insured. No yield is guaranteed.

## 8. Intelligence

### MIRDT — Market Intelligence and Risk Disclosure Terminal

Status: Live

MIRDT is a probabilistic trend-following analysis system for digital assets and US equities. Each setup includes an entry zone (low/high), invalidation price, thesis summary, confidence score (0-100), signal Z-score, and probabilistic outcome prices (P5/P50/P95).

To read a MIRDT signal:
1. Navigate to the MIRDT terminal and select an asset type (digital asset or US equity).
2. Review the entry zone — this is the price range where the setup thesis is valid.
3. Note the invalidation price — if price reaches this level, the thesis is no longer valid.
4. Read the confidence score and signal Z-score to assess the strength of the signal.
5. Review the P5/P50/P95 probability range — these are potential outcome prices at three probability levels.
6. Check the immutable data snapshot reference at the bottom for independent auditability.

All MIRDT outputs are explicitly probabilistic. This is a research tool, not investment advice.

To run a paper trade through the MIRDT Execution Model:
1. Select an active MIRDT setup and review entry zone and invalidation price.
2. Configure your paper trade: size, entry price within the zone, stop at invalidation.
3. Submit — the engine logs the trade deterministically. No AI, no discretion.
4. Track the trade in the Proof of Execution Playbook. Every trade is logged with a SHA-256 hash chain.

## 9. Cost Intelligence Engine

Accessed via Deal Intelligence — Cost Intelligence tab — Status: Live

The Cost Intelligence Engine produces institutional-grade rehab cost estimates grounded in the Craftsman National Construction Estimator (NCE) database with regional adjustments and full line-item audit trails.

To build a cost estimate:
1. Open a deal and navigate to the Cost Intelligence tab.
2. Select property type (SFR or Multifamily) and region — the regional pricing factor applies automatically.
3. Select a built-in template or build a custom scope. Templates: Light Cosmetic Rehab, Unit Turn (MF), Medium Value-Add Rehab, Heavy Gut Rehab, Kitchen Renovation, Bathroom Renovation, Exterior Refresh, and Full Systems Replacement (MF).
4. For each scope item, specify the trade and condition level (light, medium, or full replace).
5. Click Generate Estimate. The engine maps each item to the nearest Craftsman NCE benchmark, applies regional pricing, adds waste and contingency, and produces a full line-item breakdown in under one second.

Output includes grand total, per-unit and per-sqft costs, rehab-to-ARV ratio (above 35% is flagged high-risk), trade breakdown chart, and confidence badges (green 80%+, yellow 60-79%, red below 60%).

## 10. Field Intelligence

Accessed via Deal Intelligence — Field Intelligence tab — Status: Live

To conduct a walk inspection:
1. Open a deal and navigate to Field Intelligence, then click New Walk Session.
2. Select property type: SFR (16 systems: interior paint, flooring, kitchen, bathrooms, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, water heater, windows, doors, roof, foundation, garage, landscaping, and general cleanup) or Multifamily (18 systems, replacing garage and landscaping with common areas and laundry room).
3. For each system, select the condition: Light (cosmetic only), Medium (needs repair), or Full Replace. The Craftsman mid-range cost for that system appears immediately.
4. Click Generate Scope after the walk. The deterministic engine maps conditions to Craftsman benchmarks, applies regional pricing, and delivers the full scope in under one second — no AI involved.
5. Click Scope Brief to generate a 4-5 sentence professional field report narrative from the deterministic numbers — suitable for lenders or investors.
6. Review the Anomaly Detection badge — scope items 35% above or below portfolio median for that system are flagged.
7. Use the Deal Assistant tab for AI chat grounded in your deal assumptions, metrics, risks, and scope.

## 12. Proof of Execution

Status: Live

The Proof of Execution system links MIRDT signals, paper trading, and real-world project outcomes into a verifiable track record.

To submit a project outcome:
1. After a project completes, navigate to Founder Ops and open the Outcomes tab.
2. Submit actual results: final rehab cost, timeline in days, sale price or stabilized rent, DSCR, and supporting evidence documents.
3. The outcome moves through four stages: Submitted, Under Review, Approved (AXM rewards issued), or Rejected (reason documented).

Verified outcomes improve the network's validated dataset — refining Cost Intelligence Engine regional accuracy, calibrating MIRDT, and building a transparent track record. AXM rewards create an incentive for accurate, evidence-backed reporting.
