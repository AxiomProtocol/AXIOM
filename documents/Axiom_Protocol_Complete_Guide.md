## 1. Overview

Axiom Protocol is governance-first wealth infrastructure for real asset acquisition through community capital formation. It provides the tools, data, and on-chain financial infrastructure that allow everyday people to participate in institutional-grade real estate investment — from initial property discovery through community-governed land ownership. The platform is operational on Arbitrum One, supported by 72 verified automated control layers auditable at arbiscan.io.

Most community wealth-building groups fail at scale not because of bad intentions, but because they lack the infrastructure to succeed. They operate on spreadsheets, group chats, and informal trust. When capital gets involved, coordination breaks down and records disappear. Axiom replaces that informal layer with programmable, auditable, and transparent financial infrastructure.

Three operating principles guide every product:

Transparency — every capital movement, governance decision, and operational action is recorded with full audit trails. Verifiable records replace informal trust.

Coordination — structure and shared rules create reliable collaboration. Defined roles, accountability loops, and evidence-based processes replace ad-hoc decision making.

Discipline — measured onboarding, fixed treasury allocation policies, and institutional-grade reporting. Execution over narrative.

Axiom Protocol is not a bank, broker-dealer, or registered investment advisor. Participation in any product requires independent evaluation of all disclosures at `/disclosure`.

## 2. Who This Is For

Axiom Protocol was designed for Black and Latino W-2 earners aged 25-40 in Atlanta, Houston, and Charlotte who are ready to build real wealth through disciplined, structured participation in real estate — and have historically been excluded from institutional-grade capital structures.

The platform also serves real estate professionals building deal pipelines, accredited investors seeking structured real asset exposure, syndicators managing LP offerings, institutional allocators evaluating the protocol, and community organizers building shared capital infrastructure.

## 3. Getting Started

## 6. Community

### The Wealth Practice

Route: `/wealth-practice` — Status: Staged Rollout

The Wealth Practice is a structured group savings and allocation program with transparent controls, scheduling, and audit trails. Members form groups of defined size, make regular contributions, and receive rotating capital access through a three-stage trust pipeline: Forming (group organizing, rules established), Active (contributions collected, rotations executed on schedule), and Graduated (full cycle complete — unlocks Capital Program access).

To join or start a group:
1. Navigate to `/wealth-practice` and connect your wallet.
2. Browse existing groups seeking members, or click New Group to define contribution amount, cycle length, and rotation method.
3. Apply to an existing group or configure your new group with minimum membership requirements.
4. Once accepted and the group activates, contributions begin on the defined schedule.
5. Capital is disbursed to each member according to the agreed rotation, with every event recorded on-chain.

The Wealth Practice is a structured savings framework, not an investment product. It is not FDIC insured. No yield is guaranteed.

### Land Acquisition Pipeline

Route: `/land` — Status: Staged Rollout

A full real estate acquisition lifecycle platform with on-chain governance. Properties move through four stages: Identified, Due Diligence, Approved (by community vote), and Acquired.

To participate in governance:
1. Navigate to `/land` and connect your wallet.
2. Review active property candidates in the pipeline with their public summaries and due diligence status.
3. When a proposal is open for vote, cast your AXM-weighted vote for or against the acquisition within the proposal window.
4. Contribute to the community funding pool for candidates you support. Progress toward the acquisition target is tracked in real time.

Axiom's active initiative covers a targeted pipeline of 6+ acres with USDA-supported development planning. Specific acquisitions are subject to market conditions, governance approval, and regulatory requirements.

## 9. Cost Intelligence Engine

Accessed via Deal Intelligence — Cost Intelligence tab — Status: Live

To build a Craftsman NCE cost estimate:
1. Open a deal and navigate to the Cost Intelligence tab.
2. Select property type (SFR or Multifamily) and region. The regional pricing factor is applied automatically.
3. Select a template or build a custom scope. Built-in templates: Light Cosmetic Rehab, Unit Turn (MF), Medium Value-Add Rehab, Heavy Gut Rehab, Kitchen Renovation, Bathroom Renovation, Exterior Refresh, Full Systems Replacement (MF).
4. For each scope item, specify the trade and condition level (light, medium, or full replace).
5. Click Generate Estimate. The engine maps each item to the nearest Craftsman NCE benchmark, applies the regional factor, adds waste and contingency, and produces a complete line-item breakdown in under one second.

Output includes grand total, per-unit and per-sqft costs, rehab-to-ARV ratio (above 35% flagged high-risk), trade breakdown chart, and a confidence badge per line (green 80%+, yellow 60-79%, red below 60%).

Regional pricing across 14 markets: Atlanta 0.90x, Charlotte 0.88x, Houston 0.88x, Dallas 0.90x, Phoenix 0.92x, Chicago 1.05x, Detroit 0.95x, Philadelphia 1.12x, Boston 1.18x, New York City 1.30x, Los Angeles 1.20x, San Francisco 1.25x, Seattle 1.10x, National Average 1.00x.

After project completion, log actuals through the benchmarks API to build validated regional accuracy data over time.

## 10. Field Intelligence

Accessed via Deal Intelligence — Field Intelligence tab — Status: Live

To conduct a walk inspection:
1. Open a deal and navigate to the Field Intelligence tab.
2. Click New Walk Session.
3. Select property type: SFR (16 systems) or Multifamily (18 systems).
4. For each system, select the condition level: Light (cosmetic only), Medium (needs repair), or Full Replace. The Craftsman mid-range cost for that system appears immediately as you select a condition.
5. After completing the walk, click Generate Scope. The deterministic engine reads all conditions, maps to Craftsman benchmarks, applies regional pricing, and produces the full scope document in under one second — no AI, no estimation.
6. Click Scope Brief to generate a 4-5 sentence professional field report narrative written by AI from the deterministic numbers — suitable for lenders, partners, or investors.
7. Review the Anomaly Detection badge — any scope item 35% above or below the portfolio median for that system is flagged for review.
8. Use the Deal Assistant tab for AI chat grounded in your deal assumptions, metrics, risks, and scope data.

## 12. Proof of Execution

Status: Live

The Proof of Execution system links MIRDT signals, paper trading, and real-world project outcomes into a verifiable track record.

Every paper trade in the MIRDT Execution Model is logged with a SHA-256 hash chain. Trades are matched to the MIRDT setups that generated them.

To submit a project outcome:
1. After a project completes, navigate to Founder Ops or the Proof of Execution section.
2. Submit actual results: final rehab cost, timeline in days, sale price or stabilized rent, DSCR, and supporting evidence documents.
3. The outcome moves through four stages: Submitted, Under Review, Approved (AXM rewards issued to the submitting wallet), or Rejected (reason documented).

Verified outcomes improve the network's validated dataset — refining Cost Intelligence Engine regional accuracy, informing MIRDT calibration, and building a transparent track record. AXM rewards create an economic incentive for accurate, evidence-backed reporting.
