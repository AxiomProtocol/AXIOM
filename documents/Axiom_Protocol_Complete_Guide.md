## 1. Overview

Axiom Protocol is governance-first wealth infrastructure for real asset acquisition through community capital formation. It provides the tools, data, and on-chain financial infrastructure that allow everyday people to participate in institutional-grade real estate investment — from initial property discovery through community-governed land ownership.

The platform is operational on Arbitrum One, supported by 72 verified automated control layers auditable on-chain at arbiscan.io.

Most community wealth-building groups fail at scale not because of bad intentions, but because they lack the infrastructure to succeed. They operate on spreadsheets, group chats, and informal trust. When capital gets involved, coordination breaks down and records disappear. Axiom replaces that informal layer with programmable, auditable, and transparent financial infrastructure.

Three operating principles guide every product on the platform:

Transparency — every capital movement, governance decision, and operational action is recorded with full audit trails. Verifiable records replace informal trust.

Coordination — structure and shared rules create reliable collaboration. Defined roles, accountability loops, and evidence-based processes replace ad-hoc decision making.

Discipline — measured onboarding, fixed treasury allocation policies, and institutional-grade reporting. Execution over narrative.

Axiom Protocol is not a bank, broker-dealer, or registered investment advisor. Participation in any financial product requires independent evaluation of all disclosures at `/disclosure`.

## 2. Who This Is For

Axiom Protocol was designed for Black and Latino W-2 earners aged 25-40 in Atlanta, Houston, and Charlotte who are ready to build real wealth through disciplined, structured participation in real estate — and have historically been excluded from institutional-grade capital structures.

The platform also serves real estate professionals building deal pipelines, accredited investors seeking structured real asset exposure, syndicators managing LP offerings, institutional allocators evaluating the protocol, and community organizers building shared capital infrastructure.

Navigate to axiomprotocol.app in any browser. No download required.

AXM is an ERC-20 governance and fee-routing token live on Arbitrum One. Contract: `0x864F9c6f50dC5Bd244F5002F1B0873Cd80e2539D`.

AXM governs treasury allocation policy decisions, protocol upgrade proposals, capital program parameters, and land acquisition votes. It is acquired through the Axiom Exchange (DEX) at `/dex`.

## 5. Community

### The Wealth Practice

Route: `/wealth-practice` — Status: Staged Rollout

The Wealth Practice is a structured group savings and allocation program built on the traditional cooperative savings model, modernized with governance controls and institutional audit trails. Groups make regular contributions, and members receive rotating capital access through a three-stage trust pipeline: Forming (group organizing, rules established), Active (contributions collected on schedule, rotations executed), and Graduated (full cycle completed — unlocks Capital Program access).

To join: connect your wallet, browse existing groups or create one with your contribution amount, cycle length, and rotation method. Once accepted, contributions begin on schedule and capital is disbursed according to the agreed rotation. All activity is recorded and verifiable.

The Wealth Practice is a structured savings framework, not an investment product. It is not FDIC insured. No yield is guaranteed.

### Land Acquisition Pipeline

Route: `/land` — Status: Staged Rollout

A full real estate acquisition lifecycle platform with on-chain governance. Properties move through four stages: Identified, Due Diligence, Approved (by community governance vote), and Acquired. AXM holders vote on acquisitions within defined proposal windows. Community funding pools track contributions toward each acquisition target.

Axiom's active initiative covers a targeted land acquisition pipeline of 6+ acres with USDA-supported development planning. Specific acquisitions are subject to market conditions, governance approval, and regulatory requirements.

## 6. Products

### 6a. Property Analysis

Route: `/property` — Status: Live — Pricing: Free | Base ($4.99) | Premium ($14.99)

Enter any US property address and receive a structured data-driven report. Free tier: value and rent ranges, rehab cost bands, confidence score, risk flags, Census neighborhood context. Base tier adds tighter valuation, property specs, tax and sale history, and a deal grade (A-F). Premium adds rental comparables (RentCast), Walk Score, Transit Score, and full neighborhood analysis. Data sources: Census Bureau, FHFA, OpenStreetMap, RentCast, Walk Score.

### 6b. Deal Intelligence Workspace

Route: `/deal-intelligence` — Status: Live

The institutional underwriting engine at the center of the platform. Create a deal from any address — the system enriches it with verified property data automatically. Select your strategy (BRRRR, Fix and Flip, Buy and Hold, Note, Multifamily), then build financial scenarios with full control over acquisition, financing, income, and expense inputs.

The workspace includes seven tabs: Overview (property profile, IVCEE risk analysis, AI advisory), Scenarios (multiple underwriting scenarios side by side), Field Intelligence (walk inspection sessions), Cost Intelligence (Craftsman NCE cost estimates), Deal Assistant (AI chat grounded in deal data), Documents (AI-powered document extraction), and Due Diligence (structured DD checklist).

IVCEE — the Institutional Viability and Capital Efficiency Engine — provides six risk analysis layers: probability model, stress tests, sensitivity matrix, refinance risk modeling, downside protection, and capital efficiency scoring versus the portfolio.

After underwriting, the AI advisor delivers a verdict (Strong Proceed / Conditional Proceed / Hold / Reject), recommended offer strategy with negotiation leverage, creative financing structures, and a path to viability for marginal deals.

### 6c. Deal Flow — Distressed Property Feed

Route: `/distressed-feed` — Status: Live

A live aggregated feed of distressed properties from HUD, Fannie Mae HomePath, Freddie Mac HomeSteps, USDA Rural Development, Tax Sales, Sheriff Sales, and wholesaler submissions. Filter by state, city, distress type, property type, price range, and sort order. Set a Buy Box once — the system flags matching deals automatically. Every listing has an Analyze Deal button that sends the property directly to the Deal Intelligence workspace with one click.

## 8. Cost Intelligence Engine

Accessed via: Deal Intelligence Workspace — Cost Intelligence tab — Status: Live

The Cost Intelligence Engine produces institutional-grade construction cost estimates grounded in the Craftsman National Construction Estimator (NCE) database, with regional adjustments and full line-item audit trails.

To build a cost estimate: open a deal, go to the Cost Intelligence tab, select property type (SFR or Multifamily) and region, then choose from eight built-in scope templates or build a custom scope.

Built-in templates: Light Cosmetic Rehab, Unit Turn (MF), Medium Value-Add Rehab, Heavy Gut Rehab, Kitchen Renovation, Bathroom Renovation, Exterior Refresh, and Full Systems Replacement (MF).

For each scope item, specify the trade and condition level (light, medium, or full replace). Click Generate Estimate — the engine maps each item to the nearest Craftsman NCE benchmark, applies the regional factor, adds waste and contingency, and produces a complete line-item breakdown. Output includes grand total, per-unit and per-square-foot costs, rehab-to-ARV ratio (above 35% is flagged high-risk), a trade breakdown chart, and a confidence badge per line (green 80%+, yellow 60-79%, red below 60%).

Regional pricing is applied automatically across 14 markets: Atlanta 0.90x, Charlotte and Houston 0.88x, Dallas and Phoenix 0.90-0.92x, Chicago 1.05x, Philadelphia 1.12x, Boston 1.18x, Los Angeles 1.20x, Seattle 1.10x, San Francisco 1.25x, New York City 1.30x, with National Average at 1.00x.

After project completion, log actual costs through the benchmarks API. The system computes variance between the estimate, contractor bids, and actuals — building validated accuracy data for Southeast and South-Central markets over time.

## 9. Field Intelligence

Accessed via: Deal Intelligence Workspace — Field Intelligence tab — Status: Live

The walk inspection system logs property condition system by system during a physical inspection, with Craftsman NCE mid-range costs displayed in real time as each condition is selected.

To start: open a deal, navigate to Field Intelligence, and click New Walk Session. Select the property type — SFR (16 systems: interior paint, flooring, kitchen, bathrooms, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, water heater, windows, doors, roof, foundation, garage, landscaping, and general cleanup) or Multifamily (18 systems, substituting common areas and laundry room for garage and landscaping).

For each system, select the condition level: Light (cosmetic only, functional), Medium (functional but needs repair), or Full Replace (complete replacement needed). The Craftsman mid-range cost for that system appears immediately as you select a condition.

After completing the walk, click Generate Scope to run the deterministic scope engine — no AI, just the math from your conditions, Craftsman benchmarks, and regional pricing. The full scope document is ready in under one second.

Click Scope Brief to generate an AI-written 4-5 sentence professional field report narrative summarizing the findings in plain language suitable for a lender, partner, or investor. The AI writes the narrative from the deterministic numbers — it does not change them.

The Anomaly Detection badge flags any scope item whose cost falls 35% or more above or below the portfolio median for that system. The Deal Assistant tab supports AI chat grounded in your deal assumptions, metrics, risks, and scope data.

## 11. Proof of Execution

Status: Live

The Proof of Execution system links market intelligence (MIRDT signals), paper trading, and real-world project outcomes into a single verifiable track record.

Every paper trade in the MIRDT Execution Model is logged with a SHA-256 hash chain. Trades are matched back to the MIRDT setups that generated them, creating an auditable chain from signal to execution to result.

When a real project completes, the operator submits actual results: final rehab cost, timeline in days, sale price or stabilized rent, DSCR, and supporting evidence. The submission moves through four stages: Submitted, Under Review, Approved (AXM rewards issued), or Rejected (reason documented).

Every verified outcome improves the network's validated dataset — refining the Cost Intelligence Engine's regional accuracy, informing MIRDT calibration, and building a transparent track record of disciplined execution. AXM rewards for verified data create an economic incentive for accurate, evidence-backed reporting.
