## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. Short-Term vs Long-Term Thinking (2 min)
- Poverty thinks about TODAY
- Middle class thinks about NEXT YEAR
- Wealth thinks about NEXT GENERATION
- Every decision: "How does this affect my grandchildren?"
- This one shift changes EVERYTHING
- You're planting trees you'll never sit under

### 2. Breaking Generational Poverty (2 min)
- Poverty is passed down - but so is wealth
- Someone has to be the CHAIN BREAKER
- It's going to be YOU
- Your sacrifice today = your grandchildren's inheritance
- "My family has always been broke" - UNTIL NOW
- You're the turning point

### 3. The Four Pillars of Generational Wealth (3 min)
- FINANCIAL LITERACY: Teach your children about money
- ASSETS: Own things that appreciate (real estate, investments)
- BUSINESS: Create income streams you can pass down
- VALUES: Pass down mindset, discipline, wisdom
- All four are necessary
- Money without wisdom is lost in one generation

### 4. Estate Planning Basics (2 min)
- A will: Who gets what when you're gone
- Life insurance: Protection for your family
- Trusts: Protect assets from taxes and mismanagement
- Beneficiaries: Keep them updated
- Have the conversations: Tell your family the plan
- Many lose wealth because of no planning

### 5. Teaching the Next Generation (2 min)
- Don't hide money conversations from kids
- Let them see you budget, save, invest
- Give them their own money to manage (early!)
- Teach them: "Money is a tool, not a goal"
- Model discipline, generosity, wisdom
- Your example teaches more than your words
