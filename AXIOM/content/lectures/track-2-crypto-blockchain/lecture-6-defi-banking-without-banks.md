## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. What is DeFi? (2 min)
- Traditional finance (TradFi): Banks, brokers, intermediaries
- Decentralized finance: Smart contracts replace middlemen
- Open 24/7, 365 days
- Accessible to anyone with internet
- No permission needed
- Higher yields, lower fees

### 2. Core DeFi Services (3 min)
- Lending/Borrowing: Deposit crypto, earn interest. Or borrow against your assets.
- Decentralized Exchanges (DEX): Swap tokens without an exchange company
- Liquidity Pools: Provide assets for others to trade, earn fees
- Yield Farming: Optimize across protocols for best returns
- Staking: Lock tokens to secure network, earn rewards

### 3. How Lending Works (2 min)
- Deposit USDC, earn 5-10% APY
- Compare to bank: 0.01% savings account
- Your funds are lent to borrowers automatically
- Smart contract handles everything
- Withdraw anytime
- This is what Axiom Treasury enables

### 4. SUSU + DeFi (2 min)
- Traditional SUSU: Rotating savings, community trust
- On-chain SUSU: Smart contract enforces rules
- Contributions are transparent
- Payouts are automatic
- Default insurance from protocol
- Ancient wisdom + modern technology

### 5. Risks to Understand (2 min)
- Smart contract bugs (code can have flaws)
- Impermanent loss in liquidity pools
- Volatility of crypto assets
- Rug pulls (dishonest projects)
- Start small, learn, then scale
- Only invest what you can afford to lose
