# Lecture 2.1: What is Money, Really?

## Duration: 8-12 minutes

---

---

## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. The History of Money (2 min)
- Barter system: Trade goods directly (inefficient)
- Problem: "Double coincidence of wants"
- Solution: Find something everyone agrees has value
- Shells, beads, salt, cattle - all were money
- Money is just a shared belief

### 2. The Three Functions of Money (2 min)
- Medium of Exchange: Buy and sell stuff
- Store of Value: Save for later
- Unit of Account: Measure worth
- Good money does all three well
- Bad money fails at one or more

### 3. From Gold to Paper (2 min)
- Gold: Scarce, durable, divisible - great money
- Problem: Heavy, hard to carry
- Solution: Paper backed by gold
- 1971: Nixon ends gold standard
- Now: "Fiat" money - backed by government trust

### 4. The Problem with Fiat (2 min)
- Governments can print unlimited amounts
- More printing = less value (inflation)
- $1 in 1970 = $0.14 today in purchasing power
- Your savings lose value sitting in the bank
- The system rewards debt, punishes saving

### 5. Enter Digital Money (2 min)
- What if money could be:
  - Scarce like gold (can't print more)
  - Portable like digital files
  - Trustless (no government needed)
  - Transparent (everyone can verify)
- This is what Bitcoin created in 2009
- A new form of money for the internet age
