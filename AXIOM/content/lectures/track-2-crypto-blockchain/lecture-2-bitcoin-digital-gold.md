# Lecture 2.2: Bitcoin - Digital Gold Explained

## Duration: 8-12 minutes

---

## HOOK (30 seconds)
"In 2010, someone paid 10,000 Bitcoin for two pizzas. Today, those same Bitcoin would be worth over $400 million. What is Bitcoin, and why do people call it digital gold? Let's break it down."

---

## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. The Origin Story (2 min)
- 2008: Financial crisis, banks failing
- Anonymous creator: Satoshi Nakamoto
- Published a whitepaper describing "peer-to-peer electronic cash"
- January 2009: First Bitcoin block mined
- A response to broken financial system

### 2. Why "Digital Gold"? (2 min)
- Scarce: Only 21 million will ever exist
- Can't be counterfeited or duplicated
- Divisible: Can own 0.00000001 Bitcoin
- Durable: Exists as long as internet exists
- Portable: Send anywhere in minutes
- Gold properties, but better

### 3. How Bitcoin Works (Simple Version) (3 min)
- No bank in the middle
- Transactions recorded on a public ledger (blockchain)
- Thousands of computers verify each transaction
- No single point of failure
- Can't be shut down or controlled
- "Be your own bank"

### 4. Store of Value vs Currency (2 min)
- Some use Bitcoin to buy things
- Most use it as savings - "HODL"
- Over time, Bitcoin has gained value
- From $0 to tens of thousands of dollars
- Volatility decreasing as adoption grows

### 5. Why It Matters (2 min)
- Financial freedom from government control
- Accessible to anyone with a phone
- Can't be frozen or seized easily
- 2 billion unbanked people now have options
- Wealth that crosses borders
