# Lecture 3.5: Green Power - Money as a Tool for Good

## Duration: 8-12 minutes

---

## HOOK (30 seconds)
"Some people think money is dirty. They think wealth is sinful. But let me tell you something Rev Ike used to say: 'The lack of money is the root of all evil.' Today I'm teaching you about GREEN POWER - and why your wealth is a BLESSING to the world."

---

## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. The Misquoted Scripture (2 min)
- People say: "Money is the root of all evil"
- Bible actually says: "LOVE of money is the root of all evil"
- Big difference!
- Money itself is neutral - just a tool
- A hammer can build a house or break a window
- Money is the same

### 2. What Poverty Really Costs (2 min)
- Poverty creates desperation, crime, broken families
- Poverty limits education, health, opportunity
- Poverty passes from generation to generation
- There's nothing noble about struggle
- Wealthy people can help more
- "You can't feed the hungry if your own stomach is empty"

### 3. Green Power Explained (3 min)
- Rev Ike's teaching: "Money makes things happen"
- Want to help the homeless? Takes money
- Want to fund education? Takes money
- Want to heal your family? Takes money
- Want to build community? Takes money
- Money is POWER to do GOOD
- "Get some GREEN POWER in your life!"

### 4. Your Wealth Blesses Others (2 min)
- When you're wealthy, you:
  - Employ people
  - Support businesses
  - Fund causes
  - Help family
  - Model possibilities
- Your wealth creates ripples
- Getting rich is not selfish - it's SERVICE

### 5. Reframe Your Desire for Wealth (2 min)
- Don't want money for money's sake
- Want money for what it ENABLES
- Freedom to help
- Freedom to give
- Freedom to live your purpose
- Your desire for wealth is HOLY

---

## AFFIRMATION
"Money is GOOD. Wealth is a BLESSING. My prosperity serves the world. The more I have, the more I can give. I am a BLESSING generator. GREEN POWER flows through me!"

---

## CALL TO ACTION
"Make a list: 'When I'm wealthy, I will...' List every person you'll help, every cause you'll support, every dream you'll fund. Let this list FUEL your wealth journey. You're not just building wealth for you - you're building it for THEM. Comment: Who will you help when you're wealthy?"

---

## CLOSING (30 seconds)
"Money doesn't change you - it reveals you. A generous broke person becomes a generous rich person. A stingy broke person becomes a stingy rich person. Get GREEN POWER and use it for GOOD. Next lesson: Visualization - acting as if your wealth is already here."
