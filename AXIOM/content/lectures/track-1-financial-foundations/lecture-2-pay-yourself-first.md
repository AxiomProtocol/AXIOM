# Lecture 1.2: The Power of Paying Yourself First

## Duration: 8-12 minutes

---

## HOOK (30 seconds)
"Most people pay everyone else first - the landlord, the electric company, the credit card - and then save what's left over. That's backwards. Today I'm teaching you the single habit that separates the wealthy from the broke: paying yourself FIRST."

---

## TEACHING POINTS

### 1. The Backwards Way Most People Handle Money (2 min)
- Income comes in
- Bills get paid
- Lifestyle expenses happen
- "I'll save what's left"
- Problem: There's never anything left
- This is the poverty cycle

### 2. The Wealthy Flip the Script (2 min)
- Income comes in
- FIRST: A percentage goes to savings/investments
- THEN: Bills get paid
- You learn to live on what remains
- This is the wealth cycle

### 3. The 10% Rule (3 min)
- Start with 10% of every dollar you earn
- "But I can't afford 10%!" - Start with 5%, or even 1%
- The amount matters less than the HABIT
- Automate it: money you don't see, you don't spend
- Over time, increase the percentage

### 4. Where Does the 10% Go? (2 min)
- Emergency fund first (3-6 months expenses)
- Then: investments, wealth-building
- SUSU circles are perfect for this - forced savings with community accountability
- The goal: make saving automatic and social

### 5. The Compound Effect Preview (2 min)
- $100/month at 7% for 30 years = $122,000
- You only put in $36,000
- Time + consistency = wealth
- Start now, not when you "make more money"
