SECTION 3: VIDEO SCRIPT - SUSU CIRCLES (45 seconds)
================================================================================

Traditional savings accounts pay you almost nothing.

Axiom SUSU circles are different.

SUSU is a rotating savings system used by communities worldwide for generations.

Here's how it works on Axiom.

You join a circle with other members. Everyone contributes the same amount each month.

Each month, one member receives the entire pool. The cycle continues until everyone has received their payout.

But we didn't stop there.

Your SUSU contributions are secured by smart contracts on Arbitrum. Fully transparent. Fully trustless.

The community pool earns yield while waiting for distribution. Insurance funds protect against defaults.

It's ancient wisdom powered by modern technology.

Start your SUSU circle today at axiomprotocol.app.
