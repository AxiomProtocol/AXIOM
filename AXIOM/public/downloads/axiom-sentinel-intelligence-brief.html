Sentinel Intelligence Brief

Market Intelligence, Risk Authorization, and Execution Infrastructure

Document Type: Executive Brief + Technical Overview Classification: Public Version: 1.0 February 2026

Axiom Protocol has built a three-layer intelligence infrastructure that scans financial markets, classifies risk regimes, authorizes capital decisions, and executes paper trades with full cryptographic audit trails. This brief describes the architecture, capabilities, and operational status of three interconnected systems: MIRDT (Market Intelligence and Risk Disclosure Terminal), Axiom Sentinel (Capital Decision and Risk Authorization Layer), and the Execution Engine (Deterministic Paper Trading System).

I. System Overview

The Sentinel Intelligence stack operates as a three-layer pipeline where each layer has a distinct responsibility and clean interfaces between them. Data flows in one direction: from market data sources through intelligence generation, to risk-gated authorization, to deterministic execution.

Intelligence Layer (MIRDT)

Scans equity and digital asset markets for probabilistic trend-following setups using SMA crossovers, momentum indicators, and volatility measurements.

Decision Layer (Sentinel)

Consumes MIRDT outputs, classifies market regimes, scores signals, calibrates confidence, and issues authorized or denied capital decisions with cryptographic audit trails.

Execution Layer (Engine)

Converts authorized decisions into deterministic, risk-managed paper trades with live price tracking, hybrid exit monitoring, and full P&L accounting.

Proof Layer (Playbook)

Tracks cumulative performance against verifiable targets, providing transparent evidence of system capability through daily P&L progression and execution metrics.

II. MIRDT — Market Intelligence and Risk Disclosure Terminal

Purpose

MIRDT is a probabilistic trend-following analysis engine that generates structured market observations called "setups." Each setup represents a statistical pattern that has historically preceded a directional price movement. All outputs are probabilistic, backward-looking, and include explicit invalidation conditions.

Data Sources

  • US Equities: Alpha Vantage OHLCV data (daily bars)
  • Digital Assets: CoinGecko OHLCV data (daily bars)
  • Live Prices: 15-second polling for real-time monitoring
  • Volatility: ATR(14) computation from AlphaVantage/CoinGecko historical data

Setup Generation Pipeline

01

Data Retrieval: OHLCV data for all configured assets is retrieved and validated.

02

Technical Computation: SMA(20), SMA(50), directional slopes, z-scores, and volatility estimates are computed for each asset.

03

Setup Generation: When statistical criteria are met (crossover, sufficient signal strength, adequate liquidity), a setup record is created with entry zone, invalidation price, confidence score, and P5/P50/P95 probability distribution.

04

Lifecycle Management: Active setups are monitored for invalidation (price breach) and expiration (horizon exceeded). Immutable data snapshot references ensure auditability.

III. Axiom Sentinel — Capital Decision and Risk Authorization Layer

Purpose

Sentinel is the unified capital decision and risk authorization layer for the Axiom Protocol. It converts MIRDT intelligence signals into cryptographically auditable capital actions. The core principle: strategy proposes, Sentinel decides, execution obeys.

Regime Classification Engine

Sentinel continuously classifies the market into one of four regimes, each with distinct behavioral implications for capital deployment:

RegimeConditionsCapital Impact
Trend UpSMA20 > SMA50, positive slopes, breadth score >= 0.66Full allocation permitted. LONG setups receive regime bonus.
Trend DownSMA20 < SMA50, negative slopes, breadth score <= 0.34Reduced allocation. SHORT setups receive regime bonus.
Range / Low VolFlat SMAs, volatility below 3%, neutral breadthStandard allocation. No regime bonus. Higher conviction required.
High Vol Dislocation20-day volatility > 3%, vol ratio > 1.5xAll non-parameter capital deployment suspended. Multiplier drops to 0.3x.

Signal Scoring Pipeline

Each MIRDT setup that enters the Sentinel pipeline undergoes multi-stage scoring:

  • Raw Confidence: Direct output from MIRDT setup generation
  • Platt Calibration: Raw confidence is adjusted using Platt scaling to better reflect historical outcomes
  • Confirmation Score: Multi-factor agreement assessment (timeframe alignment, signal persistence, volume confirmation, risk/reward ratio, liquidity adequacy)
  • Final Score: Weighted composite = (calibrated x 0.4) + (confirmation x 0.4) + (regime bonus x 0.2)
finalScore = (calibratedProb x 0.4) + (confirmationScore x 0.4) + (regimeBonus x 0.2)
Authorization Threshold: finalScore >= 0.50 required for APPROVED status

Authorization Decisions

Sentinel issues binary decisions: APPROVED or DENIED. Each decision includes a reason code, plain-language explanation, maximum authorized notional value, and a SHA-256 hash chain entry for tamper-evident auditability. Denial is a feature, not a failure — it prevents capital deployment in unfavorable conditions.

Guard Rails

Sentinel Operating Constraints

  • Guard Rail #1: No single position may exceed the configured maximum per-asset allocation
  • Guard Rail #2: Correlated exposure limits prevent concentrated sector risk
  • Guard Rail #3: High Vol Dislocation regime automatically suspends all capital deployment
  • Guard Rail #4: Circuit breaker monitors system health across Normal, Safe Mode, Defensive Mode, and Recovery Pending states
  • Guard Rail #5: Advisory mode only until post-public governance vote grants execution authority

IV. Execution Engine — Deterministic Paper Trading

Purpose

The Execution Engine converts Sentinel-authorized decisions into risk-managed paper trades with deterministic position sizing. Every aspect is algorithmic: given the same portfolio parameters and market data, the system produces identical outputs. Human confirmation is required for every trade opening.

Position Sizing

riskBudget = portfolioCapital x (riskFractionBps / 10000)
stopDistance = |entryPrice - stopPrice|
quantity = (riskBudget / stopDistance) x globalSizeMultiplier

This formula ensures the maximum loss on any trade (if stopped out) equals the risk budget. Tighter stops enable larger positions; wider stops constrain sizing. The global size multiplier provides an additional scaling lever tied to policy mode.

Hybrid Exit System

The Execution Engine implements a dual-boundary exit model that separates capital protection from thesis invalidation:

  • riskStop: Capital protection boundary computed as entry +/- k x riskDistance, where k is adaptive based on ATR-derived volatility ratio
  • invalidationLevel: Thesis invalidation from the original MIRDT setup — the point where the trade thesis is structurally wrong

The drawdown brake serves as a portfolio-level circuit breaker. When cumulative losses exceed the configured threshold (in basis points), the system prevents opening new positions until losses are recovered. This prevents catastrophic drawdown spirals.

VI. Adaptive Metrics Engine (AME)

The Adaptive Metrics Engine is the deterministic financial computation layer that underlies Sentinel's risk assessment capabilities. AME computes regime scores, policy multipliers, adaptive targets, hard brake triggers, and payout factors.

AME V2 Components

  • LSR (Leverage Safety Ratio): Measures leverage utilization relative to safe thresholds
  • RSR (Reserve Sufficiency Ratio): Evaluates reserve adequacy for liability coverage
  • VPI (Volatility Pressure Index): Quantifies market stress on the portfolio
  • Stability Score: Composite measure of overall system health
  • Hard Brake Logic: Automatic policy transitions when metrics breach critical thresholds
  • Capital Flow Waterfall: Priority-ordered routing of capital to reserves, operations, and distribution
  • SMF (Staking Multiplier Factor): Yield permissioning based on system health

AI Oracle Integration

The AME AI Oracle provides an institutional-grade interpretation layer powered by Gemini AI. It processes AME metrics through five analytical modes: regime narration, stress recommendations, tradeoff analysis, audit summaries, and comprehensive briefings. All oracle outputs are advisory and supplement the deterministic AME computations.
