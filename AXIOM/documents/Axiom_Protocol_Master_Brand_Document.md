## PART 1 — WHO WE ARE

## PART 2 — MISSION, VISION & VALUES

### Mission
To make real asset ownership investable, transparent, and governable for communities that have historically been excluded from institutional-grade capital structures.

### Vision
A sovereign digital-physical economy where community groups can acquire land and real assets using the same governance frameworks, reporting standards, and financial controls used by professional capital allocators — without giving up self-custody or community control.

### The Problem We Solve
Most wealth-building groups — savings circles, investment clubs, cooperative buyers, real estate partnerships — fail at scale because they were never given the infrastructure to succeed. They operate on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and informal trust. When capital gets involved, coordination breaks down. Axiom replaces that informal layer with programmable, auditable, and transparent financial infrastructure.

### Core Operating Principles

Transparency. Every capital movement, governance decision, and operational action is recorded with full audit trails. Verifiable records replace informal trust. No black boxes.

Coordination. Structure and shared rules create reliable collaboration. Defined roles, accountability loops, and evidence-based processes replace ad-hoc decision making.

Security. Multi-party authorization controls, audited automated control layers, and privacy by default. 72 verified smart contracts on Arbitrum One provide independent auditability.

Discipline. Measured onboarding, fixed treasury allocation policies, and institutional-grade reporting. No speculation, no hype, no shortcuts. The focus is execution over narrative.

### OPERATIONS & COMPLIANCE PRODUCTS

GRADUATED EXECUTION FRAMEWORK (GEF)
A behavior-based qualification system that gates access to live capital deployment based on demonstrated discipline during a structured paper trading period.

Tier structure (Paper, Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3):
- Participants start on PAPER — mandatory paper trading before any live access
- Tier advancement requires: minimum elapsed days on paper, minimum trade count, minimum Execution Qualification Score (EQS), maximum drawdown limits, AXM balance requirements
- EQS is computed from six sub-scores: Risk-Adjusted Return (RBAR), Discipline Score Index (DSI), Position Sizing Consistency (PSC), Volatility Risk Score (VRS), Execution Discipline Score (EDS), Risk Calibration Score (RCS)
- Suspension mechanism: automatic execution suspension on limit breaches, with stated reason
- Daily and rolling 7-day loss limits enforced per profile

Who it is for: Traders accessing Axiom's on-chain execution infrastructure, institutional review of capital access gating policies.
